Where a rotation ended the application on iOS 17.5 (ztdsp-175-m8b), 2026-11-17, package as built.
The largest texture of this simulator is 8 192 pixels.

1. The gallery itself, in its own scene, with nothing of the tests in it.

A UI test (GalleryUITests/SizeSpikeUITests) turned the device to landscape and back with the size-spike screen
open, which shows two SpectrogramViews. In three runs of six the gallery ended (ui-tests-summary.txt: runs 1 and 3,
and the last of the three runs of 22:19 to 22:20):

    com.zombietron.ztrondsp.gallery crashed in MetalViewHost.draw(in:)

That is the drawable growing on the way back of a rotation until Metal refuses its texture (finding (B) of
WP-822-findings.md). In the other three runs the gallery lived through the turn; size-spike-device.log is from
one of them.

2. The hosted suite, runs 4 (20:19) and 5 (20:40).

The application ended in the rotation cases of the acceptance test, each time at the start of a case that itself
ran a corrected rule, with the same assertion of Metal. So the surface that grew was not the one under test but
another one, built as the package is, that was still alive in the scene when the interface turned again. Which
surface that was is an attribution, not an observation: in run 4 it is taken to be the component of the as-built
case before it (out of its window, not yet released; since then a hosted window ends its surfaces when it is
closed), in run 5 a screen of the gallery that the screen test had pushed twice and that iOS 17.5 kept alive out
of sight (since then the screen test opens a screen once and waits before it closes it). Runs 6, 7 and 8 and the
run of 22:24, after both changes, passed. The rotation acceptance run alone, between runs 5 and 6, passed for all three rules.
