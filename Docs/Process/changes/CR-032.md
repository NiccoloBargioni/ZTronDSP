## What

`Docs/API/spec-map.tsv` lists every public symbol of every digest at the clause of the specification, or the change request, that names it. WP-901a.5 made internal what nothing outside its module needs (below, "Made internal"). What is left without a clause and without an introducing change request is listed here, grouped by module, with the reason it is public. A symbol another change request already adds stays under that change request.

1. **`ZTronDSPCore`**
   - `AnyStrategy`: the box a description keeps a strategy in, stock or written outside the package; `AnalysisStep.key`, `CustomKernel.key`, `CustomProcessor.key`, and in `ZTronDSPDesign` `IIRDesign.family`, `FIRDesign.method` and `DifferenceEquation.rootFinder` hold one, and EXT-1 allows `ZTronDSPDesign` no other access than `public`.
   - `AudioBuffer`: the owned, aligned storage of an `AudioBlock`; `ZTronDSPIO` (`EngineNodeTapSource`), `ZTronDSPDesign` (`Realization`, `SystemAnalysis`), the test product (`AllocationAudit`) and the external package (`ExtensionGuide`, kernel `Despike`) build with it.
   - `BiquadSection`: one second-order section as `KernelSpec.biquad(sections:precision:)` carries it to the kernel; a stage written outside the package that lowers to the stock biquad kernel builds it (EXT-2: such a stage is fused like a stock one).
   - `PipelineContent`: the protocol of what a builder body produces; `Group<Content>`, `EnvironmentReader<Content>` and the `buildEither` functions of `PipelineBuilder` are generic over it, so it is public with them (thirteen public declarations name it).
   - `subsample(_:count:by:phase:into:)`, `upsample(_:count:by:into:)` and `zeroPad(_:count:into:paddedCount:placement:)`: the allocation-free forms of the hooks §5.11.4 names (`subsampled`, `upsampled`, `zeroPadded`); a node or a kernel written outside the package calls them on the analysis thread, where the allocating forms are not allowed (RT-2), and EXT-1 says a stock stage has nothing a user's stage lacks.
2. **`ZTronDSPDesign`**
   - `DSP.maximumRateChangeFactor`: the largest factor of a rate change (4 096), which `Decimate`, `Interpolate`, `Subsample`, `Upsample` and `DSP.rateChangeTaps` check a factor against; an app bounds its own input with it instead of copying the number.
   - `DSP.rateChangeTaps(by:rejection:passband:)`: the anti-alias filter of `Decimate` and `Interpolate` (§6.12); `ZTronDSPAnalysis` designs the inversion report of `Decimate` with it (`DecimateInversion.swift`), and EXT-1 allows it no other access than `public`.
   - `DSP.savitzkyGolayCoefficients(window:polynomialOrder:derivative:)`: the design behind `SavitzkyGolay` (§6.3.6), the weights of a smoothing or derivative filter, for an FIR of the app's own; the other designs of the target (`FIRDesign`, `IIRDesign`) are public as well.
   - `DSP.savitzkyGolayMaximumWindow`: the widest window that `savitzkyGolayCoefficients`, `DSP.savitzkyGolay` and the stage accept; their documentation and their error name it.
3. **`ZTronDSPAnalysis`**
   - `ConstantQGeometry`: the bins of a constant-Q transform as a `ConstantQStrategy` is given them; the requirements of the protocol (`latency(of:hop:window:sparsity:)`, `makeNode(of:hop:…)`) take it, so a strategy written outside the package (§5.3) needs it.
   - `ConstantQKernel`: the sparse kernel of one octave, for inspection away from a pipeline and for a strategy of the app's own; the valid compile fixture `Tests/CompileFixtures/valid/OrderRulesOfConstantQAndChroma.swift` builds one.
4. **`ZTronDSPMetal`**
   - `CrossoverTable.replacing(_:with:)` (an extension of the core's type): the table with one entry replaced, by its `CrossoverEntry` (CR-016 item 14); how `CalibrationReport` builds its table, and how an app that keeps a measured table (SEL-5: crossovers are data) changes one entry.
   - `CrossoverTable.size(of:)` (an extension of the core's type): the value of one entry, by its `CrossoverEntry`; what an app reads back from a measured table.
5. **`ZTronDSPUI`**: none. Every public symbol is named by §5.4 or §8, or by CR-021 or CR-027.
6. **`ZTronDSPTesting`**: none. §5.11.4, RT-7, CLICK-1 and the rows WP-004, WP-005, WP-006, WP-016 and WP-216 name every facility of the test product, and CR-023 item 16 names `MedianOracle`.
7. **`CZTronDSPTesting`**: none (WP-005, WP-216).
8. **`CZTronDSP`**
   - `ztd_buffer_color()`: the offset that keeps the channels of a block off 4K aliasing; `ZTronDSPCore` allocates with it (`AudioBlock.swift`), and a C target has nothing between its public headers and a file of its own: a function a Swift target calls is in `include/`.
9. **`ZTronDSPIO`**: none. Every public symbol is named by §4.4 or §4.6.
10. **`ZTronDSP`** (the facade): none. Its five re-exports are §4.2.

## Made internal by WP-901a

Nothing outside `ZTronDSPCore` names them, none is used by `@inlinable` code, and no other target, the external package, a valid compile fixture or the gallery uses them:

- the twelve keys behind the derived values of the environment: `SampleRateKey`, `NyquistFrequencyKey`, `ChannelCountKey`, `BlockSizeKey`, `FrameSizeKey`, `HopSizeKey`, `FrequencyResolutionKey`, `FeatureRateKey`, `FrequencyAxisKey`, `LatencyKey`, `IsRealTimeKey`, `SourceKindKey`. An app reads those values as the properties of `DSPEnvironment` (§5.4); the three keys it can set (`ImplementationKey`, `BiquadPrecisionKey`, `ResultRateKey`, ENV-3) stay public.
- `SinkDescription`, which CR-015 item 11 lists among the additions of the pipeline: no public declaration names it. When the owner decides CR-015, its item 11 is one name shorter.

## Why

WP-901a.5: a public symbol is either named by the specification, recorded by a change request, or made internal. These are needed by another target, by code written outside the package, or by an app, for the reason each line gives.
