## What

1. Every generator loads SciPy through `Tests/Golden/generators/_oracle.py`, which puts an inert stand-in in the place
   of eight Fortran extensions of the SciPy 1.15.3 wheel that this macOS refuses to load, and of nothing else.
2. librosa (the oracle of WP-504, 512, 525 and of the references of §6.8–§6.11) and GNU Octave (which §6.14 and
   WP-910 use to run the exported scripts) are not installed on this machine, and nothing was installed. Until the
   owner approves installing them, vectors that need librosa are generated by NumPy implementations of the stated
   definitions with every argument explicit, are marked as such in the manifest, and the Octave runs are not done.

## Why

`import scipy.signal` fails on macOS 27 with the installed wheel. The eight modules are ODE integrators, COBYLA and
PROPACK; no oracle function a generator uses reaches them, a stand-in raises if anything calls it, and any other
load failure stays fatal. Installing software is the owner's decision.

## Evidence

`dlopen` reports "section '__DATA/__thread_bss' has a zero-fill section type, but offset field is not zero" for
`integrate/_dop`, `_lsoda`, `_vode`, `optimize/_cobyla` and the four `sparse/linalg/_propack` modules; the other 106
extensions load. With the loader, `buttap`, `ellipap`, `besselap`, `firwin`, `firls`, `savgol_coeffs`, `get_window`,
`sosfreqz`, `group_delay`, the `…ord` functions, `lp2bp_zpk`, `bilinear_zpk` and `iirfilter` were called successfully.

## Sections affected

§6.1 (parity references), §6.14, §10.1 (parity, export), OD-6, STG-2.
