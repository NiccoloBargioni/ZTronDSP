## What

The illustration of §5.11.1 declares `void (*process)(…)`. The ABI fixed by WP-204 declares
`uint32_t (*process)(void *state, const float *const *in, float *const *out, uint32_t channels, uint32_t frames)`,
returning the frames written. A kernel that keeps the rate returns `frames`.

## Why

The decimator, the interpolator and the subsample and zero-stuff kernels (WP-213, WP-223, WP-227) carry a phase
across blocks, so the number of frames they produce for an irregular block is known only to them.

## Evidence

`PlanTests.aRateChangeInsideAPlanCarriesItsFrameCountOn`: a halving kernel fed blocks of 7, 64, 1 and 300 frames.

## Sections affected

§5.11.1 (kernel ABI).
