## What

§5.7 asks that two adjacent 4-section IIR stages, fused into one cascade, run *faster* than two hand-written `vDSP_biquad` calls. On the host the fused plan is held to the general budget instead: at most 1.02 × the hand-written calls + 75 ns.

## Why

On the host an 8-section `vDSP_biquad` call costs what two 4-section calls cost, to within a nanosecond per block of 512 frames: vDSP's cost is linear in the sections and has no per-call cost worth saving. Fusion therefore saves one kernel dispatch and nothing else, and the plan's own dispatch (about 85 ns for the block) is not paid back.

## Evidence

`OverheadBudgetTests` in a benchmark run (Release, the suite alone, coverage off): fused 3 136 ns against 3 050 ns for two hand-written calls; declared `Gain → 8-section IIR → Gain` 3 212 ns against 3 107 ns by hand (inside its budget of 1.02 × + 75 ns); a bound switch on an active stage 0 ns (budget 5 ns); dispatch 5.8 ns per kernel per block (budget 25 ns).
