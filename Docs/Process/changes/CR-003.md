## What

§9.3 lists `unsupportedConfiguration(reason:)`, `unstableDesign(maxPoleRadius:)`, `numericalFailure(routine:info:)`,
`backendUnavailable(reason:)` and `resourceAllocationFailed(what:)` without a stage, and also says that an error raised
by a stage carries the stage's path; WP-306b wants REAL-2's failure to name its stage. The five cases take a trailing
`stage: StagePath? = nil`. The spellings of §9.3 still construct them. `DSPError.stage` reads the path of any case and
`inStage(_:)` attributes an error to a stage, which the plan compiler does for every error a `lower(_:)` throws.

## Why

The two statements of §9.3 cannot both hold with the signatures as listed.

## Evidence

`DSPErrorTests.errorsRaisedByAStageCarryItsPath`.

§9.3.
