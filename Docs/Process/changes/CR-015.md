## What

1. **`session.environment`** is the environment at the source with, as `latency`, the delay of the whole main line (ENV-4 names both).
2. **`start()` from `finished` or `failed`** prepares the source again asynchronously; the session becomes `running`, or `failed`, a moment later. A synchronous `start()` cannot await `prepare()`.
3. **A pull source that changes format** says so on `events` and ends the stream of the old format by returning 0 from `render`; the engine re-plans and asks again.
4. **A sink that an update moves** to another place in the pipeline receives nothing between the update and the exchange: it is prepared for the new frames at once, so the old plan stops writing it.
5. **An update that arrives while a plan is being faded in** lets that plan take over at once; one that arrives during pre-roll replaces the plan on its way in, once it is accepted (SWAP-4 names pre-roll only). CR-024 item 1 says what a refused update does.
6. **Frame times are content times**: the time of a block after a stage with latency is the time of the signal it describes, so a session behind a processed source starts at minus the latency of the pipeline in front.
7. **A session that is released without `detach()`** detaches itself.
8. **A source carries the attachments of one engine**; attaching it to a second engine is `sourceUnavailable`.
9. **Control wake-ups.** The analysis thread wakes the control context between turns through a dispatch source, only when an exchange, the end of a stream, dropped frames or a stream sink asked for it; the audio I/O thread still performs no system call.
10. **`DynamicPipeline`** is an immutable value: `appending(_:)` returns a new pipeline, and `append(_:)` is the mutating spelling of the same.
11. **Additions to the public surface** that the specification implies but does not name:
    - pipeline: `StageValue`, `Recipe`, `Measuring`, `StageActivation`, `BoundSwitch`, `ActivatedStage`, `ActivatedSequenceStage`, `AnalysisStep`, `ScopeChange`, `CustomKernel`, `CustomProcessor`, `SinkDescription`, `FrameFormat`, `StageValidation`;
    - results: `BoundResult` and `BoundResult.Kind`, `ScalarResultTarget`, `VectorResultTarget`, `ScalarResultValue`, `ScalarBindingTarget`, `VectorBindingTarget`;
    - feeds: `FeedCursor`, `FeedRead`, `FeedObservation`, `FeatureFrame`, `FeedSnapshot.Content`, `EnvelopePyramid`;
    - sources and engine: `SourceEventChannel`, `RingWriter`, `AnalysisScheduling`, the cases `SourceKind.derived` and `.offline`, the four cases of `RouteChange`;
    - toolkit: `MedianNetwork`.
12. **Offline `process` and bindings.** `process` is synchronous and a binding is read on the main actor only (BIND-1). Called on the main thread it reads the bound switches once, at the start, and delivers the bound results at the end. Called off it, a bound switch counts as on and bound results are not delivered. "Offline ≡ engine" (WP-323) therefore holds everywhere for a pipeline without bindings, and on the main thread for one with them. The alternatives are a synchronous hop to the main thread, which deadlocks a caller the main thread is waiting for, or an asynchronous spelling of `process`; neither is in the specification.
13. **A binding inside a stage that is its own key.** A stage value that serves as the key of its kernel or node keeps the binding of its `isActive:` alive until the next change of topology, or `detach()`. An equal update releases every binding the session reads or writes (BIND-6: the switches and results are captured anew); it does not rebuild the boxes of the plan, which is what an equal update exists to avoid.
14. **The polling period** of the analysis thread is half the duration of the engine's block and never under 1 ms. §4.3 says "half an I/O buffer"; the engine knows its own block size and not the buffer of whatever feeds a source.
15. **A stream that is over.** `stop()` then `start()` on a `finished` session prepares the source again, as `start()` from `finished` does; an attachment that starts on a source whose stream has ended gets the stream anew.
16. **An update that drops a sink** releases it before `update(_:)` returns (ATT-5): the plan on its way out stops writing it at once, as for a sink that moves (item 4).
17. **A main line that a constant suspends** (a stage that changes the domain or the rate, written with `isActive: false`) delivers nothing, as it does when a bound switch there is off; its output is declared as it would be with the stage on, so a session behind a processed source is compiled for that format and receives no block. ACT-4 says the remainder of the branch is removed and does not say what the main line then delivers.
18. **A stage that is off for good inside a switched `Group`** suspends what follows the group whatever the group's switch says: what a constant removes is removed when the plan is compiled.
19. **Column times are session times.** After `stop()` and `start()`, and after an interruption, a session's clock starts again at zero (ACT-10), so the columns a feed receives then carry times that begin again behind those it kept.
20. **A `FeatureStream` has one consumer at a time.** A second task that iterates it while the first waits for a frame gets `nil`.
21. **A stage written outside the package states its latency twice**, in its description and in the kernel or processor that runs. A plan in which the two differ is refused with `unsupportedConfiguration`.

## Why

Each is a choice the specification leaves to the implementation, or two of its sentences that cannot both be taken literally.
