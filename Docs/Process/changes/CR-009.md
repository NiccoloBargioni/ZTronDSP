## What

`FeedAxis.indices(count:)` joins the seven cases of WP-111.

## Why

A `MelCepstrum` frame (cepstral coefficients) is a real vector that a `HeatmapFeed` accepts (§5.2), and its rows are
none of the seven: they are coefficient numbers. A domain defined outside the package needs the same.

`FeedAxisTests.tempoAndIndexRows`.
