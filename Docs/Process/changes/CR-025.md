## What

The suite of EXT-4 is the package `ExternalParity/` inside the repository. It depends on the main package by path and on its three products only, re-implements `Gain` as a `SampleProcessor`, an IIR low-pass on a C kernel of its own and a mel projection as a step with its node, and holds one compiled, tested example for each row of the table of §5.11.1, which `Docs/ExtensionGuide.md` shows. `Scripts/external-parity.sh` is gate 9. Writing it from outside showed the following.

1. **Not met: "runs within 5 % of its speed" for the gain written as a `SampleProcessor`.** It takes 1.75 to 1.81 times the stock gain's time on 512-frame blocks (four launches). The low-pass on its own kernel (0.91 to 1.01) and the mel projection (0.83 to 0.97) meet the bound, and so does the same gain written on the kernel ABI (0.90 to 0.93). The cause is the processor path of the core: a processor works in place, so the block is copied first and the call goes through an existential (`Engine/Run/ProcessorKernel.swift`). The test states the miss as a known issue, so gate 9 is green and shows it. **Proposed:** an out-of-place entry for processors (item 3).
2. **A kernel or a processor of the caller's own receives no value update** (EXT-1, EXT-2, §5.5.4). `CustomKernel` and `CustomProcessor` are identified by their key alone, and the engine sends `ZTD_COMMAND_SET_VALUES` only to the stock kernel specs. A changed cutoff or amount of a user's stage therefore compiles a plan: the new kernel starts cold and is pre-rolled, and a sink behind it receives nothing for the pre-roll and the fade, where the stock `LowPass` and `Gain` compile nothing and miss nothing; an animated value (ANI-2…ANI-5) would be a plan per display frame. **Proposed:** `CustomKernel.init(name:key:values:interpolation:decimation:latencyFrames:make:)`, whose `values` stand outside the structural identity and are sent to the kernel's `command` as `ZTD_COMMAND_SET_VALUES`; and the same for `CustomProcessor`, with a refinement of `SampleProcessor` that receives the values on the analysis thread at a block boundary.
3. **A processor that works out of place** (EXT-4): an optional entry that reads the input block and writes the output block, so that no copy is made in front of it. **Proposed** as a refinement of `SampleProcessor`; the in-place entry stays.
4. **A node cannot be run, nor a plan driven block by block, on the caller's thread** (EXT-3). `FrameWriter` has no public initialiser and the offline runner is internal; the package's own tests reach them with `@testable import`, which a user's test target cannot. A user's node cannot be held in `expectNoAllocations` on the test's thread as the stock nodes are; the suite audits the engine's own thread through `ThreadAllocationAudit` instead. **Proposed:** in `ZTronDSPTesting`, `expectNoAllocationsRunning(_ pipeline:format:blockSize:blocks:crossovers:)`, on a public plan that the caller drives.
5. **The audited vDSP allow-list is not a public header** (`Sources/CZTronDSP/ztd_vdsp.h`): a kernel written outside declares the entry points it calls as non-blocking itself. **Proposed:** make it a public header.
6. **The precision the stock filter picks for `.automatic`** uses a measurement with the block size, the channel count and the implementation; the public `recommendedPrecision()` fixes them, so a user's filter can pick another precision than its twin near the −100 dB threshold (not observed in the suite). **Proposed:** `recommendedPrecision(accelerated:channelCount:blockFrames:)`.
7. **A user's `FeatureSink` is told claim, write and release only.** The stock feeds also learn of liveness (ACT-11), of a new branch (SWAP-3), of memory pressure (MEM-1), and of the frame's domain and format at the claim. The owner decides how much of this a sink written outside should see.
8. **The main line's output across a plan exchange cannot be heard from outside**: a processed source cannot be updated, and a sink behind a changed main line is not written until the exchange is over (CR-015 item 4). CLICK-1 across an update of a user's stage is therefore asserted on the gap a sink hears, not measured.
9. **ORD-4 for stages written outside.** The core's sentence for a sequence stage after the wrong domain names the kind, not the stage; a stage that wants to be named adds one unavailable builder overload of its own, as the suite's two sequence stages do (the stock sequence stages have none). The sentence for an `Emit` after the wrong domain lists the stock sinks only.
10. **Two smaller things**: `Scripts/compile-fixtures.sh` gives a client's C module no header search path (the gate passes `CPATH`); the hosted CI runs gate 9, and so its speed step, on any runner, not on the pinned benchmark runner.
11. **Found in the C layer through the suite**: the double-precision cascade is not block-size invariant bit for bit while a section's memory is below the state floor (4.5·10⁻¹³ at a peak of 5·10⁻⁵ for a 10 Hz low-pass of order 8 at 192 kHz, blocks of 1 against 512), though `ztd_kernels.h` says it is; the user's kernel does the same, so parity holds. Given to the fix cycle of the C layer.

## Why

Item 1 is a budget of §5.7 missed, which needs this record. Items 2 to 4 are what EXT-1 ("a stock stage has nothing a user's stage lacks"), EXT-2 and EXT-3 ("the tools are public") promise and the public API does not yet deliver for a stage with a kernel or a node of its own; they change shapes that §5.11.1 fixes, which only a change request may do. The rest are refinements.

## Evidence

`Scripts/external-parity.sh`, three runs from a clean derived-data folder on 2026-11-17: 108 tests in Debug with 7 known issues (the audit's control case and the six expectations that state items 1, 2 and 11), 2 valid and 23 invalid compile fixtures failing with their sentences, 15 audit tests in Release, the speed step held at its first launch. Output against the stock twins: the gain and the low-pass bit for bit (every precision and implementation, 1 to 6 channels, blocks of 1 to 4 096), the mel projection bit for bit and within 10⁻⁶ of the maximum against the one-shot projection. Allocation audits at zero, on the test's thread, on an engine and on the engine's real-time thread. A bound switch on each stage equals its twin bit for bit and meets CLICK-1. Fixtures `FINDING-ACustomKernelCannotStateItsValues` and `FINDING-ANodeCannotBeRunOutsideAPlan` hold items 2 and 4.

## Not done, and why

- The additions of items 2 to 6: they change public shapes; they are implemented on the proposed reading once the engine's fix cycle is merged, and reworked if the owner decides otherwise.
- The display example of the guide is built and compared, not hosted in a window.
- No device run; one simulator runtime.
