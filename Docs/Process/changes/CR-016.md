## What

1. **Where the colour tables come from.** The 256 colours of viridis, magma and inferno are matplotlib's (S. van der Walt, N. Smith, E. Firing; released under CC0). matplotlib is not installed on the host, so the tables were read from a copy of the same data that another application installed on the host carries (the `d3-scale-chromatic` ramps inside the MATLAB R2025b bundle), read only, nothing copied but the 768 bytes of each table. They agree with the published anchor colours and with the published polynomial fits within the fits' error; that they equal `round(255 · matplotlib)` byte for byte could not be checked offline. Replacing them is a change of data in `Sources/ZTronDSPMetal/Color/ColorMapTables.swift` and `Tests/Golden/generators/_colormaps.py`.
2. **Turbo** is the degree-5 polynomial its author published, not the 256-entry table; the two differ by up to about 1/8 at the dark ends.
3. **Register row 23 (§7.5)** says half-float quantisation is "5·10⁻⁴ relative: under 0.005 dB". For levels stored in decibels the error is relative to the level, not to the power: up to 2⁻⁵ dB below 128 dB in magnitude, 0.031 dB measured. That is a tenth of one colour step over an 80 dB range. Proposed text: "5·10⁻⁴ relative to the stored level: under 0.04 dB for levels within ±128 dB".
4. **Register row 16 (§7.5)** says the median has no arithmetic hazard. On a GPU a comparison of floats treats subnormal values as zero, so a network of float comparisons is not exact there. The kernel compares integer keys with the same order as the floats. Proposed addition to the row: "on the GPU, comparisons are made on integer keys: float comparisons flush subnormals".
5. **Along time a pixel shows the column under its centre**; along frequency it shows the exact maximum of the rows it covers, by a walk of the reduced levels (V-8 asks for the maximum over bins; §8.2 describes one level chosen by footprint, which can miss a peak that straddles two cells of that level).
6. **A frequency scale is drawn through a table** of 4 096 positions, so any `FrequencyScale` works, the stock ones included; changing the scale uploads that table (16 KB) and no column.
7. **Shaders read texels and interpolate themselves.** Whether the device filters `r16Float` linearly is recorded in `MetalCapabilities` and not relied on.
8. **The renderers take the time span in columns and pairs**; the view layer converts seconds with the feed's `featureRate`.
9. **The live waveform ring keeps its reduced levels too** (§8.2 gives the ring to the live case and the pyramid to the static one), so every zoom of a live waveform is exact. Uploads stay proportional to the pairs that are new.
10. **The GPU median works on buffers**, not textures: no limit of 8 192 on either side of a spectrogram.
11. **`PixelImage` and `OffscreenTarget` are public in `ZTronDSPMetal`**: `ZTronDSPTesting` may not import Metal (§4.2), and the tests of the view layer need both.
12. **An unattached feed, and columns not yet written, are drawn in the first colour of the map.**
13. **Shipped crossovers are unchanged.** What the host measured (reduction by SIMD from 32 elements, the lane exchange never faster through Accelerate) is one simulator host; WP-611b…d ask for the reference devices, which this environment does not have. `Calibration.measure()` returns the measured table on any device.
14. **Additions to the public surface** the specification does not name: `MetalCapabilities`, `DisplayColor`, `ListedColorMap`, `TurboColorMap`, `GrayscaleColorMap`, `LinearFrequencyScale`, `LogarithmicFrequencyScale`, `MelFrequencyScale`, `RowInterpolation`, `PixelImage`, `OffscreenTarget`, `RenderCauses`, `WaveformRenderer`, `MetalMedianFilter`, `CrossoverEntry`, `CalibrationCurve`, `CrossoverMeasurement`, `CalibrationReport`, `CalibrationCandidate`, `Calibration.search` and `Calibration.crossover`, `MetalContext.sharedBlocking()` and `waitUntilIdle()`.

## Why

Items 1 and 2: the definition of a colour map is its table, and the host could not reach the usual source. Items 3 and 4: the register states a bound the arithmetic does not give. The rest are choices the specification leaves to the implementation.

## Evidence

`ZTronDSPMetalTests`: 115 tests in 12 suites, Debug and Release, on the simulator's Metal device. `ColorMapTests` (texture bytes equal the reference tables), `HeatmapTextureTests` (read-back parity and the measured quantisation), `MetalMedianFilterTests` (subnormals and signed zeros bit for bit), `HeatmapGoldenTests` (17 fixtures, PSNR ≥ 68.7 dB), `WaveformRendererTests` (6 fixtures, pixel-exact), `CalibrationTests`. Stability notes: `Docs/Numerics/heatmap-rendering.md`, `median-metal.md`, `crossover-search.md`.

## Not done, and why

- WP-611c: the convolution and mel pairs are measured when those steps exist (M5). WP-611d: the CPU ↔ GPU median comparison waits for the CPU median kernels (WP-529).
- WP-614: the exported script equals its golden text; it was not run under GNU Octave, which is not installed.
- Nothing was run on a physical device. The simulator's Metal device has no read-write textures, no 32-bit float filtering and textures of at most 8 192.
