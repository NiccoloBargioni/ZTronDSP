## What

1. **STG-2 oracles on expanded coefficients** are not usable for digital designs of higher order (`freqz(b, a)` differs from `sosfreqz` by 4e-2 of the peak for an order-8 elliptic band-pass). The golden vectors use `sosfreqz` and the per-section group delay, summed.
2. **WP-402.** The expanded-Butterworth fixture documents an accuracy of 0.1 that no root finder can reach: the exact roots of the rounded coefficients are 0.152 from the sixteen poles. The test holds 0.25; the fixture's figure should read about 0.2.
3. **§6.3.4.** The roll-off of ±0.5 dB per octave holds against n·6.0206 dB, not against the rounded "96 dB per octave" for order 16.
4. **ZPK → SOS** is digital only; an analog cascade of odd order cannot be held in five numbers per section.
5. **Bessel** prototypes are offered up to order 25; an elliptic design whose transition band is below double resolution is rejected with `invalidParameter`.
6. **`IIRDesign.Domain`** is `.analog` or `.digital(sampleRate:)`: the digital domain carries its rate.
7. **Row 12 of §7.2** (root polish): the two Newton steps evaluate the polynomial in double-double arithmetic; with plain Horner the Wilkinson fixture misses its documented limit.
8. **`package` access** is not used in Design; its test hooks are internal and reached with `@testable import` from the target's own tests.

## Why

Each item is a measurement or a reading the implementer had to choose; the choices are stated in `Docs/Numerics/`.

## Evidence

The report of the implementer; verified in the main tree: 178 tests of `ZTronDSPDesignTests` pass in Debug and Release, and the seven generators reproduce their vectors under `Scripts/golden.sh`.
