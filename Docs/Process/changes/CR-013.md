## What

1. **WP-210** "each channel equals the mono kernel" is not bit for bit: `vDSP_biquadm` and `vDSP_biquad` are two routines. The tests hold each channel to 1e-5 on the impulse response against both mono kernels and the double-precision reference; on full-scale noise to 1e-5 of the peak (−100 dB) against the C-loop kernel and the reference, and to 5e-5 of the peak (−86 dB) against the `vDSP_biquad` kernel, which is itself up to 1.7e-5 from the reference there. Measured against the `vDSP_biquad` kernel: −97 dB, with no margin to spare, which is why the bound is not that figure.
2. **WP-208** block-size invariance holds bit for bit for blocks of a multiple of four frames with vDSP, and for any blocks with the C loop; other sizes with vDSP differ by round-off: about −100 dB on the fixture's own signals, −94.7 to −97.7 dB on full-scale noise with other seeds (blocks of 1, of 7, and irregular ones).
3. **§7.4, gain row.** `vDSP_vrampmul` is not used: its ramp does not match its documented loop bit for bit. The ramp is rendered in closed form and multiplied with `vDSP_vmul`.
4. **RT-6 and badly scaled cascades.** A section that works more than twenty decades below the signal has its memory cleared at the end of every block. A design that puts a cascade's whole gain in its first section (as the reference pairing does) therefore fails for very low cutoffs at high rates; the filter stages spread the gain over the sections when they lower to the cascade.
5. **REAL-3.** The −100 dB threshold sends the WP-208 fixture itself to double precision (single against double is −98.4 dB on `vDSP_biquad`).
6. **Partitioned FIR.** The kernel adds no latency for any block size: partial partitions are computed with a direct head plus the delay-line tail. Agreement with the direct kernel is −120.5 dB at worst over the tested cases, not the register's −139 dB (4 097 taps in partitions of 1 024, which the tests do not hold, give −118.8 dB). Most of that distance is the direct kernel's own single-precision error: against the exact convolution the partitioned kernel is at −129 to −137 dB. For a signal in the stop band the bound is relative to the input, not to the output, which is how the stress test scales it.
7. **Lane exchange.** No crossover on the host: the C loop leads at every size from 8 to 4 096.
8. **Coverage instrumentation** slows C loops about threefold; `Scripts/xc.sh` now turns it off unless it is asked for.
9. **RT-6 in `vDSP_biquadm`.** The routine keeps its memory inside its setup, where one channel's values can be neither read nor cleared. The kernel keeps it clean all the same: a channel that has been quiet for as long as the cascade has memory, and stays quiet, runs on coefficients of zero, which empty its memory within two frames, and is given exact zeros as output; it gets its coefficients back before the first block that carries signal again (fix cycle 1, finding of lens N on WP-210).
10. **WP-216, "within one block".** In a cascade the output reaches exact zero within two blocks of falling below the floor, not one: the first block ends with state that is cleared, the second is the first to be computed from it. The tests hold two.
11. **WP-226, "reads back as set".** The host raises a computation time below half the constraint to half the constraint, so the policy reads back as set in its period and its constraint; the computation time reads back as the host made it.
12. **RT-9 after a policy has been granted.** A time-constraint policy takes a thread out of its quality-of-service class, and the system lets no thread back into a class it has left. When the analysis thread has no source left, or a later request is refused, its policy is ended and it runs as a time-share thread at the priority it had before (a precedence over the time-share base), not in a class. "Quality of service only" in the engine's report means that priority. The engine asks for half its polling interval as computation time, within what the host's scheduler grants (50 µs to 50 ms, measured on the simulator host; a device may draw the line elsewhere).
13. **"Thread Sanitizer clean" (WP-202).** The sanitizer follows neither Mach mappings nor a checked `memcpy`. The ring tells it when a mapping is new and moves frames with the copy it instruments; the allocation audits, which it blinds, audit nothing under it.

## Why

Each item is a measurement that contradicts a figure or a wording of the specification; none changes an interface.
