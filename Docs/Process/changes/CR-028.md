## What

SEL-6 says that two implementations of one primitive agree "within 10⁻⁶ relative otherwise (a reduction sums in a different order)", and row 24 of §7.5 repeats the figure. With `Float` accumulators no pair of summation orders meets 10⁻⁶ at the lengths the package works with: the figure holds at the lengths of WP-114's row and not beyond.

**Proposed wording of SEL-6:** "Implementations of one primitive agree bit for bit where they perform the same operations in the same order (the element-wise ones). Two implementations of a reduction (sum, dot product, mean square, root mean square) agree within max(10⁻⁶, n·2⁻²³) of the sum of the magnitudes of its terms, n being the length: the bound of recursive summation in single precision, for two summation orders. Choosing by size never changes what a pipeline computes beyond that."

**Row 24 of §7.5**, bound: "Bit-exact for element-wise primitives; for reductions within max(10⁻⁶, n·2⁻²³) of the sum of the magnitudes".

The other reading, which is the owner's to choose instead: keep 10⁻⁶ at every length, and have both implementations sum in chunks short enough to meet it, with the partial sums added in `Double`. It costs a call into vDSP per chunk (64 to 128 samples), that is, most of what the accelerated reduction gains.

## Why

The rule cannot be met as written, and a test at the lengths of real analysis frames (4 096 samples and more) would fail for data as ordinary as a DC offset.

## Evidence

Verifier A, `Docs/Process/reports/WP-114-A.md` (A-31), reproduced by the auditor (`audit-m1-A.md`): forced `.native` against forced `.accelerate` on the same vector, relative difference — a constant 0.1 of 4 096 samples: sum 5.07·10⁻⁶, mean square 5.03·10⁻⁶, dot product 5.03·10⁻⁶; 16 384 samples: sum 2.02·10⁻⁵; 65 536 samples: sum 8.08·10⁻⁵. vDSP alone is 1.6·10⁻⁵ from the exact sum of 65 536 × 0.1. Every figure lies within the proposed bound (at 65 536: 8.1·10⁻⁵ against 7.8·10⁻³).

## Sections affected

§7.4 (SEL-6), §7.5 row 24, §10.1 (the property "implementations of one primitive agree"), WP-114, WP-115.
