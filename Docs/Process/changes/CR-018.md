## What

1. **WP-433, the choice of precision.** `.automatic` runs a cascade in single precision when it stays within −100 dB of the double-precision result, as REAL-3 says. That holds on the two signals the measurement uses; on a noise realisation it has not seen, a cascade chosen as single reaches −97.2 dB. The test holds unseen noise to −95 dB. Proposed: REAL-3 names the measurement's signals as what the −100 dB is measured on.
2. **WP-432a…c, "within 0.1 dB of the analysis".** Held for `.automatic` and `.double`. For a forced `.single` it is held where the measurement recommends single; elsewhere the deviation is recorded (0.133 dB for a band boost of order 8), because forcing single there is asking for it.
3. **WP-434, the edge modes of `SavitzkyGolay`.** A stream starts from silence, so the first (window − 1) / 2 outputs of the stage are those of `.constant(0)` whatever `edge:` says. The five modes act in the offline function `DSP.savitzkyGolay`. Honouring them at the start of a stream needs an initial history in the core's FIR kernel.
4. **WP-422 was delivered ahead of its trigger** (the owner's request), as `MatchedPeakDesign` and a `MatchedPeak` stage of its own, not as an option of `BandBoost`.
5. **A tolerance scheme is measured, not estimated.** Kaiser's estimate alone falls up to 2.65 dB short of 80 dB when a transition band touches an end of the axis. The FIR initialiser that takes a length keeps the estimate; the initialisers that take tolerances or a roll-off measure the design and lengthen it until it holds.
6. **A least-squares scheme that no length up to 4 095 taps meets** is `unsupportedConfiguration`, naming `.windowedSinc()`.
7. **`roundOffNoiseGain(precision:)` is a level**: the summed power gain from each section's rounding point to the output, times the square of the rounding unit (2⁻²⁴ or 2⁻⁵³), in decibels; infinite when a response leaves the range of a sample.
8. **`SystemAnalysis` of an FIR system above 513 taps** is `unsupportedConfiguration`: the cap of the root finder in §6.3.8.
9. **MATLAB export of an analysis**: magnitudes are floored at −160 dB (NUM-1); the only NaN is an undefined group delay.
10. **A gain of exactly 0 dB is an identity**, which F4 removes. A `Gain` that an update moves through exactly 0 dB is therefore a change of topology, made without a click by the cross-fade of SWAP-2, where its neighbours are changes of value. Observed, not changed.
11. **Additions to the public surface** the specification does not name: `FIRDesign`, `WindowedSincMethod`, `LeastSquaresMethod`, `FilterDesign`, `SavitzkyGolayEdge`, `SystemElement`, `IIRSystemElement`, `FIRSystemElement`, `IIRSystemBuilder`, `IIRSystemContent`, `FIRSystemBuilder`, `FIRSystemContent`, `SystemKind`, `RootConditions`, `MatchedPeak`, `MatchedPeakDesign`; and from the first half of the target: the family and order-strategy types (`ButterworthFamily` … `EllipticOrderStrategy`, `BesselNormalization`), `CompanionQRRootFinder`, `EqualizerDesign`, `FilterOrderEstimate`, `FilterResponse`, `MagnitudeScale`, `ShelfSlope`, `SystemDomain`. Added 2026-11-18, after the second lens of WP-403, WP-414, WP-420 and WP-421 found the list incomplete (B-BL-2, B-BL-4): `SecondOrderSections.Section` (a member of a type the specification names and leaves open), `Bandwidth` (the three forms `.hertz`, `.octaves` and `.q` of §6.3.5, whose type the specification does not name) and the function `DSP.prewarpedAngularFrequency(hertz:sampleRate:)`.

## Why

Items 1 to 3 are cells of §12 that cannot be met as written, with what was built instead. The rest are choices the specification leaves to the implementation.
