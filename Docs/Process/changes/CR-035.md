## What

§6.8 accepts the constant-Q transform and chroma with, among others, "frame-wise cosine similarity
≥ 0.98 against reference chroma on the music fixtures". With librosa 0.11.0 installed (owner,
2026-11-21) the reference chroma is librosa's, `librosa.feature.chroma_cqt` with every argument
explicit (STG-2). This change concerns only the pseudo constant-Q, `ConstantQScale`:

- **Proposed reading.** For `ConstantQScale`, the reference chroma is `librosa.feature.chroma_cqt`
  of `|librosa.pseudo_cqt(…)|` with librosa's scale of band k removed: each band multiplied by
  n_fft^(3/2) / ℓ_k, where n_fft is the frame of `librosa.pseudo_cqt` and ℓ_k the length librosa
  builds atom k with (`librosa.filters.wavelet_lengths` with librosa's relative bandwidth,
  (2^(2/B) − 1) / (2^(2/B) + 1)). The bound, 0.98, is unchanged.
- **Unchanged.** For the transform, `ConstantQ`, the reference chroma is `librosa.feature.chroma_cqt`
  of `|librosa.cqt(…)|` (`librosa.vqt` where γ > 0) as librosa computes it, and the bound is met
  as written.

Proposed text for the acceptance of §6.8: "… frame-wise cosine similarity ≥ 0.98 against reference
chroma on the music fixtures (librosa's `chroma_cqt` of `librosa.cqt`; for `ConstantQScale`, of
`librosa.pseudo_cqt` with its band scale ℓ_k / n_fft^(3/2) removed)."

## Why

The bands of `ConstantQScale` read their own amplitude: §6.8 projects magnitudes "through the
magnitude of the kernel's response", and the kernel's atoms are scaled so that a sine at a bin
centre reads its amplitude (CR-022 item 1; the tone acceptance of §6.8, within 0.5 dB). Each band
has response 1 at its centre.

`librosa.pseudo_cqt` scales them otherwise: its basis is the transform of L1-normalised atoms
multiplied by ℓ_k / n_fft, and `scale=True` divides the result by √n_fft (`scale=False` multiplies
it by ℓ_k instead). Relative to the specification's, band k carries a factor ℓ_k / n_fft^(3/2),
proportional to 1/f_k: in the fold into pitch classes the low octaves weigh more, and the chroma
differs although the bands are otherwise the same. No argument of `librosa.pseudo_cqt` gives
bands of unit response, so the bound cannot be met against librosa's chroma as librosa scales it,
for a reason that is the specification's own amplitude convention. Removing the factor is the
documented conversion: after it the chroma agree to 0.9998.

The conversion does not make the two pseudo constant-Q transforms equal, which is why the vector
of the definition stays the tested one (`ConstantQScaleTests`, 10⁻⁵ of the frame maximum) and
librosa's chroma is a further vector: librosa's atoms use its relative bandwidth (1 to 3 % longer
than with α = 2^(1/B) − 1 of §6.8), are built of 2⌊ℓ_k/2⌋ + 1 samples, and its basis is sparsified.
After the conversion the magnitudes agree to a frame-wise cosine of 0.9997 and differ by a factor
of 0.94 to 0.98 per band.

The full transform has the same kind of difference, smaller: `librosa.cqt` with `scale=True` divides
band k by √ℓ_k, so its bands carry a factor proportional to √ℓ_k (measured per band: about √N_k / 2),
and the chroma meets 0.98 as it is.

## Evidence

Measured by `Tests/Golden/generators/constant_q.py` (`Scripts/golden.sh --only constant_q`,
2026-11-22, librosa 0.11.0, NumPy 2.2.6, SciPy 1.15.3, soxr 1.1.0), every librosa argument explicit:
the least frame-wise cosine similarity of the definition's chroma against librosa's.

| Case | Fixture | librosa's chroma of | As librosa scales it | Band scale removed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `pseudo-music-semitones` (A2, 6 octaves, 12 per octave, frame 4 096, hop 512) | music | `librosa.pseudo_cqt` | 0.9630 | 0.9998 |
| `pseudo-speech-thirds-of-semitones` (C4, 4 octaves, 36 per octave, filter scale 0.75) | speech | `librosa.pseudo_cqt` | 0.9473 | 1.0000 |
| `transform-music-defaults` (C1, 7 octaves, 36 per octave, hop 512) | music | `librosa.cqt` | 0.9898 | — |
| `transform-music-variable-q` (γ = 5, 24 per octave) | music | `librosa.vqt` | 0.9974 | — |
| `transform-music-top-octave` (C6, 1 octave, 36 per octave, hop 128) | music | `librosa.cqt` | 0.9887 | — |
| `transform-speech-semitones` (tuning 0.25) | speech | `librosa.cqt` | 0.9691 | — |

Other readings of "reference chroma" for the pseudo constant-Q, measured the same way and rejected:
`librosa.feature.chroma_cqt` of `librosa.cqt` (the full transform) gives 0.8746 (music) and 0.8560
(speech): one frame of 4 096 samples does not have the time–frequency resolution of the atoms.

The package's own chroma, measured by the tests that hold it (Debug, iOS 27.0 simulator):
`ConstantQScaleTests.theChromaOfThePseudoConstantQIsThatOfLibrosa` and
`ChromaTests.theChromaOfTheMusicFixturesIsThatOfLibrosa(name:)`; the figures are in
`Docs/Process/reports/impl-librosa-oracles.md`.
