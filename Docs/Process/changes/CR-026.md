## What

1. **A source-fed component is the feed-driven one over a feed the view owns**, hosted by the mechanism of `.pipeline(_:on:)`: the pipeline it attaches is what `.processing` gives, followed by the component's recipe into that feed. There is no second mechanism, so `.processing` animates as `.pipeline(_:on:)` does (ANI-3), and every appearance modifier, axis, pinch and size transition works on a source-fed view as on a feed-driven one.
2. **The feed a view owns** keeps 1 024 columns for a heat map and 2 048 pairs for a waveform, a little over ten seconds at the defaults. The specification has no modifier for it.
3. **Which view reads which analysis modifier.** `.analysisFrame(_:hop:window:)` is read by the spectrogram and by the mel spectrogram of the tempogram, not by the chromagram or the waveform; `.melBands(_:)` by the tempogram only; `.processing(_:)` by all four. `hop` is required and `window` defaults to `.hann`. `HeatmapView` has no `init(source:)`: the table of §8.1 gives it none.
4. **`.processing` has three spellings**: a `Pipeline<Samples, Samples>`, a builder closure that gives one, and a builder closure of sequence stages, so that the specification's `.processing { HighPass(…) }` and a block that holds a `Tap` both compile.
5. **Every picture starts empty**: the view makes a new feed for another source and each time it appears. Another engine in the environment, or another analysis value, keeps the feed.
6. **The attachment stays while the scene is inactive**; only the draw scheduler pauses.
7. **A source-fed view does not publish its session** through `.dspEnvironment`.
8. **The tempogram** runs `OnsetStrength()` and `Tempogram()` with their own defaults. Its 128 mel bands need a transform of at least 768 points: `TempogramView(source:)` with a smaller frame and no `.melBands` is refused by the engine, and the refusal is logged (CR-021 item 10).
9. **ANI-3, "one update per display frame"**, is asserted as at least two updates and no more than one per display frame counted, because ANI-5 lets the newest win.
10. **Two defects of the first half's host were fixed** on the way (`Hosting/PipelineHost.swift`; no public signature changed):
    - Given another source or another engine, the host detached and attached again *to the old one*: on iOS 27 `onChange(of:perform:)` runs the closure of the evaluation before the change, so the handlers read the old values off the view. The host now watches one value (pipeline, source, engine) and reads what changed off the value handed over. The first half's test counted attachments only, and its second engine had the same block size; it now writes a block to the new source and reads the new engine's block size.
    - A second attach could start beside one still on its way (after a disappear, another source or another engine): the late session still held the sinks and the source's claim, so the new attach was refused and the view ended with no attachment. One engine operation is now on its way at a time across all of these, and a session that lands late is detached before the next attach is asked for. The price: an attach that waits in `prepare()` delays that view's next attach until it lands.
11. **What SwiftUI does on iOS 27, further to CR-021 item 14**: `onChange(of:perform:)` runs the closure of the evaluation before the change, `onAppear` and `onDisappear` the latest one; a reader of an environment key is not evaluated again when an equal value is set again.
12. **Wanted from `ZTronDSPCore`**: a feed that starts empty when it is attached anew, or a public way to clear it (a feed attached again in the same shape keeps its columns, which is why item 5 makes a new feed); the notification of a changed shape of CR-021 item 15, of which a changed `.analysisFrame` is one more case. A note: a feed's liveness flag flips before the announcement of it is queued, so a reader of the flag can be ahead of a listener.
13. **`Package.swift`**: the test target `ZTronDSPUITests` also depends on `ZTronDSPIO` and `ZTronDSPDesign`, for the tests with a file source and with `.processing` of filter stages. The production target's dependencies are unchanged.
14. **Additions to the public surface**, all named by §8.1 and VC-4: `SpectrogramView.init(source:)`, `WaveformView.init(source:)`, `ChromagramView.init(source:)`, `TempogramView.init(source:)`, `.analysisFrame(_:hop:window:)`, `.melBands(_:)`, and the three forms of `.processing`.

## Why

Items 1 to 9 are choices the specification leaves to the implementation. Item 10 corrects a merged package before its verification. Item 12 needs API outside the target.

## Evidence

`ZTronDSPUITests`: 248 tests in 18 suites (179 of the first half and 69 new), three runs in Debug and three in Release in the implementer's copy and again on the main tree with these files. One view shows a mock, a file and a processed signal in turn (the feed's strongest row is the bin of 3, 6 and 9 kHz; pitch classes 9, 0 and 4; 125, 93.75 and 112.5 beats per minute within 1; reaches of 0.5, 0.25 and 0.05), each as one attachment in the same Metal view. A hundred evaluations of the parent attach nothing, update nothing and draw nothing. Another analysis frame is one update of another topology; an equal one is none. Inside `withAnimation` the session receives interpolated values of one length with equal progress, no re-plan, and afterwards runs the target. Line coverage of the four added files and the three changed ones: every line. Twenty-five mutation checks, each caught; the two defects of item 10 each fail a test with the old code put back.

## Not done, and why

- Level 2 of the stability suite and the gallery's source-fed examples belong to the Examples project (WP-809b, WP-810).
- A hardware input with its permission prompt behind a source-fed view: only a device shows it; the "attach waits in `prepare()`" path is exercised with a gated mock.
- Nothing was run on a device, in an application, or on a system other than the iOS 27.0 simulator.
