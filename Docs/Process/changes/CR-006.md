## What

F1 says adjacent gains multiply into one gain kernel and that the fused plan is bit-identical to the unfused one. The
fused kernel is `KernelSpec.gainCascade(factors:ramp:)`: one kernel that applies the factors one after another.

## Why

`(x·g1)·g2` and `x·(g1·g2)` differ in the last bit for most factors, so a single multiplied gain cannot meet the
bit-for-bit requirement of the same rule. Applying the factors in sequence inside one kernel keeps the saving F1 is
after (one dispatch, no extra buffer) and the exactness.

## Evidence

To be recorded with WP-307's fusion test (fused ≡ unfused, bit for bit).
