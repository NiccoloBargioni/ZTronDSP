## What

1. **Normalisation of the atoms.** An atom is `(2 / Σw) · w(½ + m/N) · e^(j2πνm)`, `m = −⌊N/2⌋ … ⌈N/2⌉ − 1`: a sine at a bin centre reads its amplitude. This is not librosa's normalisation.
2. **Time.** Frame `n` is centred on input sample `n · hop` in every octave; before its first sample the signal counts as zero. The latency is the moment the lowest octave completes, and is declared: 39 335 samples at 48 kHz with the defaults (0.82 s); 0.13 s with `gamma` 4.45 Hz.
3. **One kernel per transform.** The atoms of an octave are the definition applied at that octave's own rate; with `gamma` 0 one kernel serves every octave.
4. **Sparsity is guarded by energy.** `sparsity` thins a row by share of summed magnitude, and each row keeps at least 99.9 % of its energy; a row that cannot is refused with a reason. With more than one octave a row reads bins up to 0.8 of the Nyquist frequency only, the clean band of the decimators.
5. **Half-band decimator.** A Kaiser windowed sinc through `FIRDesign`: pass band to 0.4, stop band from 0.6 of the input Nyquist frequency, 80 dB, 53 taps. The hop must be a multiple of `2^(octaves − 1)` (O-17); the error names the nearest hops that are.
6. **`ConstantQStrategy` is a protocol** with the stock value `.multirateSparseKernel`.
7. **`ConstantQScale`** projects through the unit-peak magnitude response of the atoms (squared for powers), with the defaults of `ConstantQ`, and refuses a lowest bin whose atom is longer than the frame.
8. **`ChromaFold`.** Defaults: `normalization: .max`, `threshold: 0`, `floor: 1e-10`; `ChromaNormalization` is a strategy protocol with `.none`, `.l1`, `.l2`, `.max`; a frame under the floor is twelve zeros.
9. **References (STG-2).** librosa is not installed: the golden vectors are the published definitions evaluated with NumPy (`Tests/Golden/generators/constant_q.py` says so).
10. **Wanted inside the target**: one band-projection node shared by the mel scale and the pseudo constant-Q (the second repeats the first, because an existing file could not be edited by the package that added it), and a crossover primitive of its own for the pseudo constant-Q, which reads the mel projection's.

## Why

Items 1 to 8 are choices the specification leaves to the implementation. Item 9 is what the host allows. Item 10 is a refactoring for a later package.

## Evidence

`ZTronDSPAnalysisTests`: 282 tests in 25 suites in the main tree (Debug) on 2026-11-16. Measured by the implementer: bin-centre tones within 0.008 dB over 252 bins (0.5 dB allowed); against the definition, at most 0.86 % of the excerpt's largest magnitude at sparsity 0.01; chroma, least frame-wise cosine similarity 0.9985 (0.98 asked); the half-band filter's stop band 80.4 dB down, and aliasing through the whole transform at most −94 dB; the sparse product within 0.78 ε of the sum of its terms; the pseudo constant-Q within 3.1·10⁻⁷ of the frame maximum (10⁻⁵ asked). Ten mutation checks, each caught; two of them first found weak tests, which were strengthened. Stability notes: `Docs/Numerics/constant-q-kernel.md`, `half-band-decimator.md`, `sparse-kernel-product.md`, `constant-q-transform.md`, `constant-q-scale.md`.
