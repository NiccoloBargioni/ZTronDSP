## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **N — numerical correctness.**

- Definition: §6.2, peak or RMS in dBFS over the step's own window and hop, given in seconds (defaults 50 ms and 10 ms). The conversion clamps at the floor of −160 dB (§6.1).
- Edge cases:
  - silence and an impulse;
  - full scale and Nyquist;
  - a window of one sample (1 ns, accepted as one sample);
  - times that do not fit are refused at attach time, naming `Level`: zero, negative, infinite or NaN; a hop longer than the window (`timesThatDoNotFitAreRefusedAtAttachTime`);
  - channel policies, with `.perChannel` refused as CR-019 item 1 records.
- Precision: the mean square (`Vector.meanSquare`, bound by size) and the peak (`vDSP_maxmgv`) are single-precision reductions of the whole window at every hop (`LevelAndWaveformNodes.swift:42–49`), so no running sum drifts. The decibels are computed in double precision. The result stays within 10⁻⁶ dB of a double-precision reduction (above).
- STG-1:
  - fluent modifier: `.level(_:window:hop:channels:)` and `.level(_:to:)` equal the builder;
