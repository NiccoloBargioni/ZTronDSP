## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - RT-2, by reading everything that runs on the analysis thread: `CosineTransformNode.process` (`Nodes/CepstralCoefficientNodes.swift:41-49`): one `memcpy` of the bands into the node's input, `vDSP_DCT_Execute`, the orthonormal scale (`vDSP_vsmul`) and the first coefficient's √½, one emission. `CosineBasisNode.process` (`:72-83`): one `cblas_sgemv` with the basis, plain or transposed by a flag fixed at creation, one emission. No loop of their own, no allocation, lock, system call, Objective-C message, logging or `await` that the verifier could see; whether `cblas_sgemv` hands work to other threads is not visible to the audit, which counts the calling thread (not verified).
  - The audit and the lines changed: `theNodesRunWithoutAllocating` passes in Debug and Release. A 64-byte allocation before the `memcpy` of `CosineTransformNode.process` (before `CepstralCoefficientNodes.swift:43`, gate `a512` of `VerifierB/mutations.py`) makes it fail at `CepstralCoefficientTests.swift:156` with 1 000 allocations (the one DCT); one after the guard of `CosineBasisNode.process` (after `:73`, gate `a512b`) with 3 000 (the basis forward at 40 and at 26 bands, and the inverse). Restored, it passes and the tree equals the pristine copy byte for byte.
  - RT-3: the DCT setup is made in the initialiser (`:22-35`) only after the length rule admits the band count by arithmetic (E-09, `:24`), and destroyed in `deinit` (`:37-39`); the orthonormal basis is computed once (`:69`); both nodes are made in the factories (`Steps/CepstralCoefficients.swift:46-52`, `:88-93`), which the plan compiler runs.
  - RT-5: `AlignedBuffer` throughout. RT-6: neither node keeps state between frames.
  - No hidden copies: the one visible copy is the bands into the node's own input before the DCT (`:43`), O(bands) per frame; no closure, no copy-on-write, no existential.
  - Complexity (§7.2 row 19, "DCT-II for the coefficients"): the vDSP DCT, O(B log B), for a band count the transform documents; otherwise the basis, O(B·C) per frame for B bands and C coefficients.
  - SEL-3: the implementation is chosen once, in the factory, by the length rule (`Steps/CepstralCoefficients.swift:50-51`), and the hot path holds no size test; §7.4 says "the length rule, then measured", and nothing is measured (finding 2). The orthonormal scale (`Nodes/CepstralCoefficientNodes.swift:46`) calls vDSP at every count: the question of WP-504-B, finding 2.
