## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **N — numerical correctness.**
  - Definition: §6.13. The sampling theorem: the share of the signal's energy at or above f_s / 2M, from an average of power spectra under a Kaiser window of β = 24 over half-overlapping segments, one-sided (`RateInversions.swift:76–113`), negligible at or below 10⁻¹⁰; `Decimate` adds the share between the pass-band edge and f_s / 2M, and the pass-band ripple measured on its taps (`DecimateInversion.swift:35–83`). `Decibels`: the share of values at or below the linear value of the floor (`StageInversions.swift:212–242`). `Cepstrum(.complex)`: the phase kept, no bin at or below the floor, the largest phase step between bins under π − 10⁻³ (`:75–134`). `CepstralCoefficients`: full rank, "`count` equals the band count" (§6.13), read as `count >= bands` (`:159`, A-34). The predicted error is "a bound on the relative error" (§6.13; `InversionReport.swift:127`); the relative error is the largest error over the peak, or over the maximum of its frame (`InversionReport.swift:75–78`, §6.1); the stability note says "A predicted error is useful only as a bound" (`inversion-analysis.md`, hazard 3).
  - Precision: shares in double precision; against the exact DFT +0.000000 dB (six digits); on the project's fixtures 2.4·10⁻⁸ to 1.9·10⁻⁵ dB; the leakage of the window on band-limited signals 1.0·10⁻¹⁵ (signal 2), 2.1·10⁻¹⁶ (a tone), 8.0·10⁻²⁵ (DC), all under the negligible share.
  - Tolerance: 1 dB (the row); not loosened. The project's aliasing test also holds the prediction to √(2·truth) within 12 % and the measured RMS error within 15 %.
  - Currency: `inversion-analysis.md` and row 22 of `currency-2026-11.md` ("the package's own procedure, on classical results"): current; register row 9 (rate changes) names WP-539.
  - Edge cases (`out/edge-e13-inversion-data.txt`, `out/edge-e17-cepstral-coefficients.txt`, `out/edge-e19-decibels-report-and-non-finite.txt`, `out/trap-t26.txt`, `out/trap-t29.txt`, `out/trap-t30.txt`; ratios from `out/predicted-bounds.txt`; "largest" is the relative error the type defines, "RMS" is 10^(−SNR/20)):

## Change requests filed (FIX-4)

CR-VA-1 and CR-VA-2 (`WP-513-A.md`) concern A-35, A-NB9 and A-NB11.

**CR-VA-5 — Infinities in an inversion report.**

- *What.* NUM-1 forbids NaN and infinity. The inversion reports return infinities by design: `InversionMeasurement.signalToNoiseRatio` is +∞ for a round trip that is exact bit for bit (`InversionReport.swift:70–71`), its `relativeError` is +∞ for an error held against a signal that is zero everywhere (`:113`), and `predictedError` is +∞ for silence at a floor (`StageInversions.swift:118`, `:234`). CR-019 item 10, still proposed, covers only the last.
- *Why.* An exact round trip has no finite signal-to-noise ratio, and an error against nothing has no finite relative size; the reports are right to say so, and the tests of the package rely on it (`theReportOfAnExactPairIsExact`, `aMeasurementIsWhatItsNameSays`). But no rule allows it, so a verifier cannot tell an intended infinity from an overflow: `Cepstrum(.complex, floor: 5e-324)` reports a head-room of +∞ (A-29), and `Decibels(floor: .greatestFiniteMagnitude)` a predicted error of +∞ (A-35).
- *Evidence.* `VerifierA/out/edge-e13-inversion-data.txt` (silence: predicted ∞, measured relative error ∞; exact round trips: SNR ∞), `out/trap-t30.txt`, `out/edge-e19-decibels-report-and-non-finite.txt`.
- *Proposed wording* (§6.13, after INV-4): "**INV-5** The quantities of a report are finite, with two exceptions: the signal-to-noise ratio of a round trip that is exact bit for bit is +∞, and a relative error, measured or predicted, held against a signal that is zero everywhere is +∞. NUM-1 applies to every other quantity of a report."
- *Sections affected.* §6.1 (NUM-1), §6.13.

**CR-VA-6 — Which error the predicted error bounds.**

- *What.* §6.13 calls `predictedError` "a bound on the relative error" without saying which. The package measures two: the RMS error relative to the RMS of the signal, 10^(−SNR/20), and the largest error relative to the peak, or to the maximum of its own frame for frames (`relativeError`, the measure its type documents, §6.1). Its predictions bound the second for `STFT`, `Decimate`, `Decibels`, `Cepstrum` and full-rank `CepstralCoefficients`, and its tests assert `relativeError <= predictedError` for them; for subsampling and for `CepstralCoefficients` below full rank they estimate the first, and the tests assert agreement with it within 15 % and 1 %. The stability note also exempts subnormal signals from any relative bound (hazard 4), which neither the types nor §6.13 say.
- *Why.* A caller cannot tell from a report whether its prediction bounds the error it will see. Against the documented measure, the RMS predictions are exceeded by a factor of 1.3 to 5.2 (A-31).
- *Evidence.* `VerifierA/out/predicted-bounds.txt`, from `out/check-inversion.txt`, `out/edge-e13-inversion-data.txt` and `out/edge-e17-cepstral-coefficients.txt`.
- *Proposed wording* (§6.13, the comment of the code block and a new sentence after the table): "`report.predictedError // from the conditions: an upper bound on measuredError.relativeError`" and "The predicted error bounds the largest error of the round trip relative to the peak of the signal, or to the maximum of its own frame for frames (§6.1). A signal whose peak lies below `Float.leastNormalMagnitude` is outside any relative bound; the report says so with a condition."
- *Sections affected.* §6.1, §6.13, §7.5 row 22, §12 (the rows of WP-538 and WP-539).
