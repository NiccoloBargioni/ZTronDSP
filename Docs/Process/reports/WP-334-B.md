## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - RT-2: `WaveformFeed.write` appends each pair as a column of two values to the same sequence-locked store a heat map uses: a stack pair, a `while` loop, one `memcpy` and four atomic operations per pair; no allocation, no lock. Verifier's audit (`vb334_writingAWaveformFeedAllocatesNothing`, 1 000 frames of 8 pairs with clears, Debug and Release): 0 allocations. The suite has no audit of this method (finding 1).
  - RT-4: the store is the feed's sequence-locked column store. RT-5: raw pointers; the arrays of a snapshot and a pyramid are made off the analysis thread.
  - Complexity: the pyramid is built in one pass per level, linear in the pairs (65 536 pairs: 98 µs; Release, indicative); choosing a level is linear in the levels.
  - No hidden copies on the hot path. Off it, `FeedSnapshot.minima` and `maxima` gather a row with a stride each time they are read, and `pyramid()` reads both once.
  - A benchmark is recorded: none.
