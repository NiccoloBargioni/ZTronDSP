## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - Setups are created when the kernel is, and only then: `vDSP_DFT_zrop_CreateSetup` twice in `ztd_partitioned_create` (ztd_partitioned.c:238-239), behind the creation seam, for a length admitted by arithmetic (a power of two from 16 up, E-09); destroyed in `destroy`. The kernel spectra are computed there too.
  - All memory is allocated there: P spectra of 2B floats shared by the channels, and per channel a window of 2B, a delay line of P·2B and a tail of B. `CreationFailureTests.everyKernelConstructorSurvivesEveryRefusal` refuses each of the 20 requests in turn.
  - `ztd_partitioned_spectrum` is a control-context accessor (not `ZTD_NONBLOCKING`, not on the hot path).
  - The structure is exercised under the allocation audit of WP-215 (`PartitionedKernelTests.processingIsAllocationFree`), which bites (1 000 allocations with an allocation the compiler cannot see (a call, from the hot function, to a helper in another file that does `malloc` and `free` and is declared `nonblocking`, as an allow-list entry is; tried in Debug and in Release, with the same counts) in `ztd_partitioned_process`). Benchmarks of the structure in use are recorded there (`kernel.partitioned.2001-taps.partition-512.*`).
  - `malloc(16)` written into `ztd_gain_process` stops the package build: "function with 'nonblocking' attribute must not call non-'nonblocking' function 'malloc' [-Werror,-Wfunction-effects]".
