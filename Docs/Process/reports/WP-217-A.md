## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **N — numerical correctness.** Result matches the cited definition and the golden vectors within the stated tolerance. Edge cases: empty, length 1, DC, Nyquist, silence, full scale, non-finite input (NUM-1). Precision choice. `ALG`: the currency record and the stability note, checked against the code. No tolerance loosened to make a test pass.

- Definition: out = from + (to − from)·g with g = (frames done + 1) / total, exactly 1 on the last frame. The kernel's output is bit-identical with that formula when the product and the sum are rounded once (a fused multiply-add), not when they are rounded separately (A-15).
- Edge cases: a fade of 0 frames is one frame long; frames past the end are the target; `out` may be either input (repo tests).
