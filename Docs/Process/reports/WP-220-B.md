## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **N — numerical correctness.**
  - The model: unprocessed path = the input delayed by the stage's latency (ACT-8); off: `wet + (dry − wet)·k/fade` for k = 1 … fade, then the unprocessed path; on from bypassed: the kernel reset, pre-roll for latency + settling frames with the output unchanged, then `dry + (wet − dry)·k/fade`; a fade turned around continues from where it stands. The implementation follows it in every case tried, including the one path no test of the package runs (a fade-in switched off mid-way, ztd_plan.c:359-366) and two channels fading alike.
  - Edge cases: a stage that starts bypassed, blocks of 64 and 512, a fade shorter than and equal to a block, toggles in consecutive blocks, a stage without latency or settling time, a recursive stage with settling time.
  - CLICK-1 is measured by `Click.measure` as the larger of the two steady sides; that reading is the one used here too.
  - Not `ALG`. No tolerance loosened in the suite; two of its checks do not test what the row asks (findings).
