## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.** RT-1…RT-9. The allocation audit exists and passes. No hidden copies. Complexity as registered in §7.2. A benchmark is recorded. Setups created when the plan is compiled only. Implementations bound by size (SEL-3).

- Memory orderings (`ztd_ring.c:176–312`). The writer owns `written`, the reader `consumed`; each loads its own counter relaxed, the other's with acquire, copies, then publishes with a release store. The reader therefore only copies frames whose copy-in happened before the `written` it acquired, and the writer only overwrites slots whose copy-out happened before the `consumed` it acquired; a stale view of the other counter under-estimates space or availability and is safe. Counters are 64-bit frame counts and never wrap. `dropped` is the writer's alone, atomic, read by anyone.
- RT-8: a full ring drops the newest frames, counts them, never blocks (no system call, no lock, no loop that depends on the other thread) and never grows. RT-1: every function of both ends is `ZTD_NONBLOCKING`; `ztd_ring_write_waiting` and `ztd_ring_reset` are not, on purpose.
- Allocation audit: `RingTests.bothEndsAreAllocationFree` (both storages, 1 000 rounds), passes in [D] and [R], fails under [HEAP]. Allocation happens in `ztd_ring_create` only.
- Benchmark recorded: `ring.plain.write-read.512x2` 116.5 ns, `ring.mirrored.write-read.512x2` 116.5 ns ([BENCH]).
