## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **A — API, architecture, platform.**

- Shape: `Measuring<Step>` is a `PipelineStage` with `Input == Output == Step.Input`, constrained to steps that produce a scalar-valued domain; `StageDescription.measuring(_:to:isActive:)` is what a step family builds its `Step(…, to:)` initialiser from.
