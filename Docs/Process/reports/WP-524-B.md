  - What runs on the analysis thread: `ChromaFoldNode.process` (`Nodes/ChromaNodes.swift:36-71`) and, through it, the normalisation's `norm(of:)` (`Steps/ChromaFold.swift:20`, `:28-36`, `:44-52`, `:60-68`, or an app's own); `reset` does nothing (`:73`). `ChromaCQT` adds nothing of its own: it expands to `Tap { ConstantQ; Magnitude; ChromaFold; Emit }` (`Recipes/ChromaRecipes.swift:36-43`, `:52-59`).
