## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **N — numerical correctness.**
  - Definition: the minimum and maximum of IEEE 754-2019 (a NaN in either lane makes both NaN; −0 below +0). Verified as above; edge cases: empty lanes, length 1, the four-lane boundary (3, 4, 5), the 1 032-frame chunk of the vDSP form (1 031, 1 032, 1 033), ties, zeros of both signs, non-finite values.
  - `ALG`. Currency record: present, dated 2026-11-15, names the registered source (§7.2 row 16) and says that no search was made (finding 2). Stability note `Docs/Numerics/lane-exchange.md` against the code: the rule, the FMIN / FMAX body on arm64 and the scalar tail are as it describes; its timing table agrees with this run in order of magnitude and in its conclusion. Its stress test fails when the mitigation is removed, as it claims: with the scalar exchange reduced to a plain compare-and-swap (ztd_lane_exchange.c:22-37) `theImplementationsAgreeOnTiesAndValuesThatAreNotNumbers` fails in all six cases.
  - Precision: Float32 lanes, no arithmetic. No tolerance in these tests to loosen (comparisons are on bit patterns, with every NaN folded onto one).
