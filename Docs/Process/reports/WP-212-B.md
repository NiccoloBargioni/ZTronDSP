## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - `process`, `command` and `reset` are `ZTD_NONBLOCKING`, defined in a target built with function-effect errors (the check bites: `malloc(16)` written into `ztd_gain_process` stops the package build: "function with 'nonblocking' attribute must not call non-'nonblocking' function 'malloc' [-Werror,-Wfunction-effects]"). Read against RT-2: no allocation, no lock, no system call, no logging; every loop is bounded by the frames, the channels or a size fixed at creation.
  - Allocation audit: `processingAndTapChangesAreAllocationFree` (1 000 blocks of 37 and 512 frames, 20 tap changes, fades inside fades) passes in Debug and Release. It bites: with an allocation the compiler cannot see (a call, from the hot function, to a helper in another file that does `malloc` and `free` and is declared `nonblocking`, as an allow-list entry is; tried in Debug and in Release, with the same counts) in `ztd_fir_process`, `ztd_fir_command` and `ztd_crossfade_mix` it reports 1 376 allocations.
  - Everything is allocated in `ztd_fir_create` (history, two tap sets, two 64-frame cells); `vDSP_conv` needs no setup.
  - Cost: O(taps) per sample when blocks are whole cells of 64 frames. `kernel.fir.255-taps.512` 3955 ns against 3445 ns for the bare `vDSP_conv` on the same work (about 15 % for the history and the copy out). Benchmarks recorded: `kernel.fir.16-taps.512` 796, `.64-taps.512` 1338, `.255-taps.512` 3955, `.255-taps.512-in-blocks-of-48` 8048, `.2001-taps.512` 33477 ns.

## Findings (VER-4)

| # | Checklist item | Location | Evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | R — complexity as registered (§7.2 row 7, O(K) per sample) and "no hidden copies" | Sources/CZTronDSP/ztd_fir.c:30-52; ztd_internal.h:99-123 | Cost against the block size (Release, serial run, ns for 512 input frames; the verifier's probe `VerifierBProbes.r_timings`): 255 taps in blocks of 512: 3966; 128: 3968; 64: 6919; 32: 7511; 16: 14585; 7: 36482; 1: 228518. The kernel computes a whole 64-frame cell into its own buffer for every block that touches the cell and copies the frames owed: a block of n < 64 frames costs about 64 / n times the registered work (58 times at one frame), and every output frame is copied once more. This is what buys bit-for-bit block-size invariance; it is stated in ztd_kernels.h:94-96 and ztd_internal.h, and is in no change request. (Blocks of exactly 64 frames came out at 1.7 times the 512 figure in this run, the same in the interpolator; not explained, not pursued.) | Non-blocking |
