## Lens N

- **Definition.** Independent (`ref.py`, NumPy, from §6.8 and CR-022 item 1: atoms normalised so that a sine at a bin centre reads its amplitude; nothing imported from the generator).
- **Precision.** The kernel is computed in `Double` (atoms, transforms, energies, thresholds) and stored in `Float` for the product. Right: the energy criterion compares sums of squares down to 10⁻³ of a row, and the threshold search needs those sums exact to far better than `Float`'s 6·10⁻⁸; storing in `Float` costs 4.6·10⁻⁸ of a row's largest entry, below the product's own `Float` error (WP-520).
- **Edge cases** (`VA_Edges`, `VA_T04`, `VA_T05`):
  - *empty / length 1*: `applied` to an empty spectrum and to a spectrum of one bin gives 36 zero coefficients (no trap); a geometry of one bin per octave is refused with its reason.
  - *DC, Nyquist*: a row reads no bin at 0 Hz or at the Nyquist frequency of its octave unless its atom does; the 0.97-of-Nyquist octave keeps its energy, the 0.85-over-two-octaves one is refused (decimators' transition band).
  - *silence*: `applied` to zeros and to ±0 gives zeros; subnormal spectra give zeros.
  - *full scale and beyond*: `applied` (double) to ±greatestFiniteMagnitude everywhere and alternating gives finite coefficients (peaks 2.2·10³⁴ and 5.0·10³⁶).
  - *non-finite input*: one NaN, +∞ or −∞ bin that no row reads gives finite coefficients; a NaN in a bin a row reads propagates (non-finite in, non-finite out: replaced by 0 at the source adapter by NUM-1, not graded).
  - *Int arguments* (`octave`, `transformLength`: Int.max, Int.min, 0, −1 and lengths around the longest atom): all refused with reasons; *Double* (`sparsity`, Kaiser β, Gaussian σ: ±greatestFiniteMagnitude, subnormal, ±0, NaN, ±∞): refused, or a finite kernel (sparsity subnormal and ±0 keep every entry). No trap.
