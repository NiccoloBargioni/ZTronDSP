## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.** RT-1…RT-9. The allocation audit exists and passes. No hidden copies. Complexity as registered in §7.2. A benchmark is recorded. Setups created when the plan is compiled only. Implementations bound by size (SEL-3).

- ACT-5: an active switched stage pays two pointer / state tests per block, plus one copy of the block when it has latency (`ztd_plan.c:239–243`); nothing is compiled or allocated when the switch moves (`ztd_switch_command` is non-blocking).
- ACT-7: off fades to the unprocessed signal and then stops calling the kernel; on resets, pre-rolls for latency + settling frames with the output discarded, then fades in; a turn-around in mid-fade continues from where the fade stands. The fade length is the caller's argument: "10 ms, never shorter than one block" is not enforced in C (stated in `ztd_plan.h:49`).
- The delay line and the unprocessed block of a stage with latency are allocated in `ztd_plan_make_switchable` (control context).
- The tests move the switch with `ztd_plan_command`; the queue (WP-206) and the plan are joined by the engine's runner, not in C.
- The tree's switch tests are mono. Verifier's own, three channels (`VerifierAMultichannelSwitch`): every channel of a switched 201-tap FIR with latency gives, bit for bit, what that channel gives alone, through off, on, off again during the pre-roll and a turn-around in mid-fade, for blocks of 64, 480 and 512; a switched stage behind a decimator works on the decimated blocks.
