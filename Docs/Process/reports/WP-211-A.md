## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **N — numerical correctness.** Result matches the cited definition and the golden vectors within the stated tolerance. Edge cases: empty, length 1, DC, Nyquist, silence, full scale, non-finite input (NUM-1). Precision choice. `ALG`: the currency record and the stability note, checked against the code. No tolerance loosened to make a test pass.

- Definition: the kernel is the transposed direct form II in double with every product and sum rounded on its own, rounded to Float32 on the way out: bit-identical with an independent Python loop (6 000 frames of noise) and with itself for blocks of 1, 7, 512, irregular and one block, on two cascades. Against `sosfilt` it is within −145 dB, not bit-identical: SciPy's build fuses multiply-adds, the kernel does not (`#pragma STDC FP_CONTRACT OFF`).
- Edge cases against `sosfilt`: DC, Nyquist, a full-scale square, a single frame within −144 dB; silence gives exact zeros; a NaN stays in the state until `reset`.
- Stability note against the code: with the state narrowed to Float32 the acceptance test fails at six of its fifteen frequencies, as the note says; restored. CR-013 item 4 (RT-6 clears a section that works twenty decades below the signal) describes the code and its test.
- Precision: double state and coefficients, Float32 samples (REAL-3).
