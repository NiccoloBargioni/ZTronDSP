## Findings (VER-4)

| # | Checklist item | Location | Evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-12 | Lens N, precision choice | `Sources/ZTronDSPAnalysis/Nodes/CepstrumNodes.swift:51–52` (an absolute floor of 10⁻¹⁰ under the logarithm) | See the list after this table | non-blocking |

What the evidence for A-12 shows:

- At 110 Hz the acceptance is met by a margin of 0.026 %: 0.974 % against 1 %. The definition in double precision gives 0.626 % on the same frames.
- The difference is the single-precision transform. At 110 Hz, 1 780 of the 2 049 bins of a frame lie below 10⁻⁷ of the frame's largest bin: they are round-off, and the absolute floor of 10⁻¹⁰ does not reach them.
- Their logarithms move every quefrency (`cepstrum.md`, hazard 2). The package's cepstrum of the first frame differs from the double-precision definition by 5.2 % of its maximum, and from the same definition computed from a single-precision transform by 0.7 % (`pitch_precision.py`).
- CR-019 item 13 records the narrow margin.

Suggested resolution: a floor relative to the frame's largest bin, at the transform's own floor (about 10⁻⁷ of it, `real-fft.md`), would make the pitch independent of round-off.
