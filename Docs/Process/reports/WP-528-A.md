## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **N — numerical correctness.**

- Definition: §7.2 row 16, a selection network pruned to the median's lane.
- The code builds Batcher's merge exchange (Knuth's algorithm 5.2.2M, `MedianNetwork.swift:51–75`) and prunes it backwards to the exchanges the median lane depends on (`:77–88`). `median-network.md` describes it as the odd-even merge sort for the next power of two with the lanes that do not exist removed. The two describe the same network: the merge exchange on k lanes is the network for the next power of two, without the exchanges that touch the missing lanes. The measurements above hold either way.
- Edge cases:
  - one lane (no exchange);
  - counts that are even, below 1 or above 255 are refused (`aCountThatHasNoMedianLaneIsRefused`);
  - ties, and zeros of both signs;
  - values at the ends of the range and below the normal range (`theMedianOfEveryStressSignalIsTheSortedMiddle`).
- A NaN makes the result unspecified, as documented (`:91–92`); NUM-1 keeps NaN out at the source adapter (§6.1).
- Precision: no arithmetic; comparisons and exchanges only, so the result is one of the inputs, exactly.
- `ALG`:
  - Currency: `currency-2026-11.md` row 16 (current; the two-dimensional method of Adams 2021 is noted with its patent, CR-020 item 2), and the first lines of `median-network.md`.
  - Stability note checked against the code. The mutation the note names (one exchange dropped from the pruned network, here the first) makes `MedianNetworkTests.theMedianOfEveryStressSignalIsTheSortedMiddle` fail with 13 issues. `everyZeroOneInputPutsTheMedianOnItsLane` (7), `randomDataGivesTheSortedMiddle` (192) and `theNetworkIsPrunedToWhatTheMedianNeeds` (7) fail with it (group M1). Restored, byte for byte.
- Not a stage, so STG-1 does not apply. No tolerance applies: the tests ask for equality.
