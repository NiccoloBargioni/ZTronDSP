## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **N — numerical correctness.**

- Definition: a pair of level k + 1 is the minimum of two minima and the maximum of two maxima; a last pair without a partner is carried up alone (`FeedSnapshot.swift:99-110`). Minimum and maximum are exact operations, so equality with the direct reduction is bit for bit, and the test holds it so.
- Edge cases: no pair (no level, tested), one pair (one level, by reading), arrays of unequal length (cut to the shorter, by reading), an odd count (tested with 301).
- Non-finite input cannot reach the feed (NUM-1 at the adapter); `min` and `max` on NaN would not be symmetric, which is why that matters.
- Time of a pair: the frame's time plus the pair's index over the feature rate (`WaveformFeed.swift:74, 86`); not asserted by a test.
