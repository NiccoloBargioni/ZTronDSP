## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - RT-2, by reading everything that runs on the analysis thread: `WaveformNode.process` (`Nodes/LevelAndWaveformNodes.swift:94-141`) walks the block in chunks through the channel mixer; each step takes what is left of the open stretch or of the chunk (at least one sample), reduces it with `vDSP_minv` and `vDSP_maxv`, folds it into the stretch's extremes and, when the stretch is full, writes the pair into the output frame; the frame is emitted once per block. The frame holds `blockFrames / bucket + 2` pairs (`Steps/Waveform.swift:50`), enough for the largest block; the guard that would emit a full frame early (`:124-127`) cannot be reached by construction and is marked `coverage:ignore`. Loops bounded by the block size; no allocation, lock, system call, Objective-C message, logging or `await`.
  - The audit and the lines changed: `theNodeRunsWithoutAllocating` passes in Debug and Release. A 64-byte allocation at the top of `WaveformNode.process` (before `LevelAndWaveformNodes.swift:96`, gate `a536` of `VerifierB/mutations.py`) makes it fail at `LevelAndWaveformTests.swift:254` (2 000 allocations, one per block of each tap) and `:259` (1 000); one in the mixing path of the channel mixer (gate `a504b`, see WP-504-B) makes the stereo audit fail at `:259` (1 000) while the mono one passes. Restored, all pass and the tree equals the pristine copy byte for byte.
  - RT-3: the node holds the mixer and scalars only; the mixer's scratch is made in its initialiser, inside the step's factory (`Steps/Waveform.swift:54-56`), and the frame's capacity is fixed when the step is lowered (`:50`); a stretch is limited to 2²⁸ samples (`:46`).
  - RT-5: raw pointers into the block and the frame. RT-6: the state carried between blocks is the open stretch's count and extremes (copies of samples) and two integers.
  - No hidden copies: the samples are read where they are; the mixer copies only to form the mean of several channels.
  - Complexity: no row of §7.2; O(1) per sample.
  - SEL-3: nothing is chosen at run time and the hot path holds no size test; the least and the greatest of a stretch are reductions of §7.4 (`Primitive.reduction` names minimum and maximum, and `Vector.Bound` provides them), called through vDSP at every stretch length (finding 2).
  - A benchmark is recorded: none (finding 1).
