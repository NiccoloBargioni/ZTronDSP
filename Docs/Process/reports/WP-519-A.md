## Lens N

- **Definition.** The filter against its own specification (§6.12 and the note: pass band to 0.4, stop band from 0.6 of the octave's Nyquist frequency, 80 dB), recomputed from the taps; the hop rule against §6.8 ("divisible by 2^(octaves − 1)").
- **Precision.** Designed in `Double` (`FIRDesign`), run in `Float` by the C decimator. Right: 80 dB is 10⁻⁴ in amplitude, three orders above `Float`'s 6·10⁻⁸; the response measured from the taps the package runs keeps −80.38 dB.
- **Edge cases** (`VA_Edges`, `VA_T06`, `VA_T08`, `VA_T13`; trap probes each in a process of its own):
  - *empty, length 1*: the transform of an empty signal and of one sample gives no frame, no trap (WP-521's probe, through the decimators).
  - *DC*: a constant passes at its own level (the stress test); *Nyquist*: a full-scale tone at the Nyquist frequency leaves −95 dB down (stress test, run).
  - *silence, subnormals*: exact zeros (stress test, run; and my probe of the transform of ±0 and subnormal signals: all exactly 0).
  - *full scale and beyond*: one sample of 10¹⁰ … 10³⁵ through the decimator gives finite output, peak A/2 (`edges.json`).
  - *non-finite input*: replaced by 0 at the source adapter (NUM-1); a signal with NaN and ±∞ gives the transform of the zeroed signal exactly.
  - *state across blocks*: block sizes 1, 37, 512, 1 024, 1 025, 4 096 give the transform of 441-sample blocks bit for bit (WP-522a's probe).
  - *the hop* (Int.min, −1, 0, 1, 63, 100, 32, 2²⁴, 2²⁴ + 64, Int.max − 63, Int.max, Int.max − 1, and 26 octaves): **traps near Int.max (A-4); a suggestion that is not legal for 26 octaves and more (A-5)**; everything else refused with a reason.
