## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - What of the package runs on the analysis thread: `SparseKernelProduct.apply` → `multiply` (`Nodes/ConstantQNodes.swift:52-90`), once per completed octave frame, from `ConstantQNode.transform` (`:393-397`).
  - RT-2: the loop reads and writes raw pointers only; no allocation, lock, log, `await` or Objective-C message; `multiply` is `static` and `@inline(__always)`, so there is no dispatch at all. RT-3: the four buffers are made in `init` (`:27-47`) when the node is made (control context). RT-5: `AlignedBuffer`s; the kernel's arrays are not read on the analysis thread. RT-6: the product keeps no state ("A product may serve several octaves of one node: it keeps no state"; `theProductRunsWithoutAllocating` asserts the same result after 1 000 passes). RT-1, RT-4, RT-8, RT-9: not applicable.
  - RT-7 layer 2: `multiply` carries `@_noAllocation @_noLocks` behind `ZTRONDSP_PERF_ANNOTATIONS` (`:65-67`); `Scripts/check-perf-annotations.sh` passes: the package builds with the setting on, and its own fixture that allocates fails to build (`VerifierB/logs/perf-annotations.txt`).
  - RT-7 layer 3, allocation audit: `ConstantQKernelTests/theProductRunsWithoutAllocating()` (1 000 products inside `expectNoAllocations`): passes in Debug (runs 1, 2) and Release (run 4). With one allocation added at the head of `apply` (`ConstantQNodes.swift:57`, injection 11) the audit fails (`ConstantQKernelTests.swift:319`), and so do the node audits that run the product (`ConstantQTests.swift:383` and `:388`, `ChromaTests.swift:301`): 4 tests, 4 issues (`mut-11.log`).
  - No hidden copies: no array, closure or existential on the path; columns are `Int32` read from an aligned buffer.
  - Complexity (§7.2 row 13): one pass over the kept entries of the rows, O(entries) per octave frame; a row is about a dozen terms, where a plain loop leads a library call (E-26). Accelerate's complex sparse routines are iOS 18.5 and later (E-11), above the deployment target, so the loop is the one implementation; there is no size choice to bind (SEL-3 not applicable), and the dense product exists only in the tests, as the reference and in the benchmark.
  - Setups at compile time: the product is made once per kernel in `ConstantQNode.init` (`:277-288`), and one product serves every octave when `gamma` is 0.
  - Currency (ALG): `Docs/Numerics/sparse-kernel-product.md` opens with the currency check of 2026-11-15 and points to `currency-2026-11.md` row 13 ("Current", one more alternative to register: CR-020 item 3). The record is consistent; no network was available to check the literature itself.
  - Noted for lens N, not a finding of this lens: hazard 3 of the note (`sparse-kernel-product.md:38`) says "the transform of finite samples is finite"; the transform of samples at ±greatestFiniteMagnitude is not (finding B-7 of WP-521 and WP-522a). The product itself turns finite spectra into finite coefficients (the probe above).
