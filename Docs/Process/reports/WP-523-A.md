- **Definition.** Independent (`ref.py`, NumPy, from §6.8 and the projection's comment: the band is \|W(f_b − f_k)\| / W(0), the unit-gain atom's response; the thinning as `ConstantQAtom.threshold` states it).
- **Precision.** Weights in `Double`, stored in `Float`; bands summed in `Float` (scalar, 8-lane and vDSP dot products, or `cblas_sgemv`). Right for the package: every term is non-negative (the order rule admits magnitudes and powers only), so nothing cancels and a band is within about n·ε of its own value; measured 6.47·10⁻⁷ of the frame maximum, below the 10⁻⁵ asked. ## Change requests filed (FIX-4)

**CR-A2 (proposed): which dense projection the row of WP-523 compares with.**

- *What.* The M5 row of WP-523 asks "≡ the dense projection computed in double precision, within 10⁻⁵ of the frame maximum". `ConstantQScale` thins each band by `sparsity` (default 0.01) as a kernel row is thinned. Against the projection through its own thinned weights the step is within 2.75·10⁻⁷ at sparsity 0.01; against the projection through the whole response it is 8.34·10⁻³ of the frame maximum at sparsity 0.01 and 6.47·10⁻⁷ at sparsity 0. The repository's comparison with the dense projection (`thePseudoConstantQOfTheFixturesEqualsTheDenseProjection`, and the note's figures of 3.1·10⁻⁷ and 2.4·10⁻⁷) is made at sparsity 0; the stress test compares the nodes at the default sparsity with the step's own thinned weights summed in double, so what the thinning costs against the whole response is bounded by no test.
- *Why.* Read as the whole response at the default sparsity, the row is not met (8.34·10⁻³ against 10⁻⁵), and by design: `sparsity` is "the share of a band's summed weights that may be dropped" (`ConstantQScale.swift:48`), 1 % by default, and a band of a flat spectrum loses up to that share. Read as the step's own weights, it is an equivalence of implementations, met at both sparsities probed, but then nothing in the row bounds what the thinning costs. The next verifier should not have to choose.
- *Proposed wording* (M5 table, WP-523): "≡ the dense projection of the same weights computed in double precision, within 10⁻⁵ of the frame maximum, at every sparsity; with sparsity 0, ≡ the projection through the whole response within 10⁻⁵; a bin-centre tone peaks in its bin; powers use the squared response". If the owner means the whole response at the default sparsity instead, the default of `sparsity` or the tolerance has to change.
- *Evidence.* `VerifierA/out/ref-results-alignment-pseudo-chroma.json`, key `523.dense` (per case in its `value`).
- *Sections affected.* §6.8 (`ConstantQScale`), §12 M5 table row WP-523; `Docs/Numerics/constant-q-scale.md`.
