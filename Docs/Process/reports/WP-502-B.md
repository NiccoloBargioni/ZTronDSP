## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - RT-2, by reading everything that runs on the analysis thread: `VDSPFFTPlan.forward` and `inverse` (`Nodes/TransformPlans.swift:52-79`) and `DoubleDFT.forward` and `inverse` (`:128-153`) are two `vDSP_vsmul(D)` that split or join the even and odd samples, one `vDSP_DFT_Execute(D)`, two `memcpy` and scalar stores: no allocation, lock, system call, Objective-C message, logging or `await`, and no loop of their own. A plan is held as `any FFTPlan`, a class-bound protocol: one witness-table call per frame, no existential box. Dynamic dispatch is per frame, never per sample (after a framing step the frame is the plan's unit: each frame emitted is processed downstream at once, core `Nodes/AnalysisNode.swift:117-136`).
  - The audit and the line changed: `aPlanTransformsWithoutAllocating` passes in Debug (4 runs) and Release (1 run). A 64-byte allocation added as the first statement of `VDSPFFTPlan.forward` (after `TransformPlans.swift:52`; gate `a502` of `VerifierB/mutations.py`, run by `VerifierB/run-gates.sh a502`) makes it fail at `TransformTests.swift:139`: "expected no heap traffic, found 1000 allocation(s) of 64000 byte(s)". Restored, it passes, and the tree equals the verifier's pristine copy byte for byte.
  - RT-3: the four work buffers and both setups are made in the initialisers (`:25-45`, `:97-117`), which run when a plan is compiled: `backend.makePlan(length:)` is called inside the node factories of `STFT` (`Steps/STFT.swift:118`) and `InverseSTFT` (`Steps/InverseSTFT.swift:121`), `DoubleDFT(length:)` inside the initialisers of the cepstral nodes (`Nodes/CepstrumNodes.swift:31`, `:130`), and the plan compiler runs the factories (core `Engine/ExecutionPlan.swift:458`). The setups are destroyed in `deinit` (`:47-50`, `:119-122`), with the node. `TransformSetup.willCreate` (`Transform/FFTBackend.swift:98-104`) reads a task-local in the initialiser only. The one-shot `DSP.realDFT` and `DSP.inverseRealDFT` (`Transform/DFT.swift`) allocate by contract (§9.2) and no node calls them.
  - RT-5: every buffer handed to vDSP is an `AlignedBuffer` of the plan's; no `Array` on the path. RT-6: a plan keeps no state between calls.
  - No hidden copies: the split into even and odd samples is the input `vDSP_DFT_zrop` takes, and the result is copied from the plan's buffers into the caller's (two `memcpy` of N/2 values); the factor ½ and the factor 1/N are applied inside those same passes (§6.1). No closure, no copy-on-write, no box.
  - Complexity (§7.2 row 1): the vDSP DFT, O(N log N), on the lengths 2ⁿ and f·2ⁿ (f of 3, 5 or 15, n ≥ 4) through `DSP.isFastLength` (core `Toolkit/Padding.swift:9-15`), as registered; admission by arithmetic only (E-09).
  - Setups made when the plan is compiled only, never per block: as above; the audit would see a setup made per block as an allocation.
  - SEL-3: nothing to bind by size: a backend has one implementation, chosen where the step is declared (a strategy, §5.3); the plan is bound to its length when the plan is compiled.
  - A benchmark is recorded: none (finding 1).
