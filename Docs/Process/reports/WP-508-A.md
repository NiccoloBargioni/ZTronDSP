## Findings (VER-4)

| # | Checklist item | Location | Evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-5 | §6.11 acceptance statement, "conversions round-trip within 10⁻⁹ relative for both formulas" (also "exact inverses … in double precision"). A result that is wrong | `Sources/ZTronDSPCore/Units/MelFormula.swift:44` (`2_595 * Foundation.log10(1 + hertz / 700)`) and `:49` (`700 * (Foundation.pow(10, mel / 2_595) - 1)`); the test `Tests/ZTronDSPAnalysisTests/MelTests.swift:22–24` | See the list after this table | blocking |

What the evidence for A-5 shows:

1. **Measured in the package.** The HTK round trip hertz(mel(f)) is off by 3.83·10⁻⁸ relative at f = 10⁻⁶ Hz and by 7.25·10⁻⁹ at 10⁻⁵ Hz (probe; `.build/vlogs/probe-run4.log`: "hertz(mel(1e-06)) = 1.00000003833145e-06").
2. **Measured in NumPy.** `VerifierA/python/htk_round_trip.py` writes the same formula in double precision: 4.45·10⁻⁵ relative at 10⁻⁹ Hz, 5.26·10⁻¹⁰ at 10⁻⁴ Hz (passes), 6.6·10⁻¹⁴ at 1 Hz.
3. **The cause.** `1 + f/700` and `10^x − 1` cancel for small f. Written with `log1p` and `expm1` the error is at most 1.7·10⁻¹⁶ at every one of those frequencies.
4. **Why the test does not find it.** The test's HTK line compares with `.absolute(1e-9 * max(frequency, 1))`: relative only from 1 Hz up. Its stride (0, 37.3, 74.6, … Hz) samples no frequency between 0 and 37.3 Hz.
5. **Who else claims it.** The protocol's comment (`MelFormula.swift:3–4`, "exact inverses") and the stability note (`mel-filter-bank.md:18`) claim the round trip without a limit.

Reproduction, as in `VerifierA/README.md` §1:

```
cp VerifierA/probes/VerifierAProbeTests.swift Tests/ZTronDSPAnalysisTests/
TEST_RUNNER_VA_OUT="$PWD/VerifierA/out" ZTD_DEVICE=ztdsp-27-vm5aa Scripts/xc.sh test ZTronDSPAnalysisTests/VerifierAProbes
rm Tests/ZTronDSPAnalysisTests/VerifierAProbeTests.swift
ZTD_DEVICE=ztdsp-27-vm5aa Scripts/xc.sh build
```

The two cases of `findingTheHTKRoundTripIsNotWithin1e9RelativeAtSmallFrequencies(hertz:)` fail, and the log names the values. Or, without a build, run `python3 VerifierA/python/htk_round_trip.py`.

Resolution of A-5:

- Write the HTK pair as `2_595 / ln 10 · log1p(f / 700)` and `700 · expm1(m · ln 10 / 2_595)`.
- Make the test relative everywhere, and add frequencies below 1 Hz (for example 10⁻⁹, 10⁻⁶ and 10⁻³ Hz).

A-5 also holds WP-535, whose row's acceptance is the whole of §6.11's (`WP-535-A.md`). The same fix resolves it there.
