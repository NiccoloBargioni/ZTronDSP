  - Typed errors: every resolution is `throws(DSPError)`.
  - Concurrency (§9.2): Swift 6 language mode (`Package.swift:26`); no `@unchecked Sendable` and no `nonisolated(unsafe)` in the files.
  - DEP and platform: imports Foundation only; nothing above iOS 15, no `@available`; no UIKit, Combine or `AnyView`.
  - VIS-2: the 18 public declarations of `Frequency.swift` are documented, the threading contract at the type ("an immutable value, usable on any thread"); in `MelFormula.swift` 6 of 15 have no comment of their own (B-15).
