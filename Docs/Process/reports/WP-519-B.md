## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - What of the package runs on the analysis thread: `HalfBandDecimator.process` and `reset` (`Nodes/ConstantQNodes.swift:142-150`): two pointer stores and one call into the C decimator of WP-213 (`Sources/CZTronDSP/ztd_rate.c`) through the kernel's function pointer, once per octave per chunk of at most 1 024 samples. `ConstantQSchedule` is arithmetic done when the node is made.
  - RT-1, RT-7 layer 1: the decimator's loop is C, `ztd_decimator_process` and `ztd_decimator_reset` declared `ZTD_NONBLOCKING` (`ztd_rate.c:23-24`, `:53`), and the target is compiled with function-effect warnings as errors (`Package.swift:45-46`); every build of this verification succeeded. RT-2: no allocation, lock, log or `await` in `process`/`reset`; the indirect call is once per octave per chunk, not per sample (see the change request in WP-521-B.md on the wording of RT-2). RT-3: the C kernel, its two channel-pointer cells (`ConstantQNodes.swift:124-127`) and the `halved` buffer of each octave are made in `makeNode` (control context) and released in `deinit` (`:130-136`); the half-band taps are designed once (`HalfBand.taps()`, `ConstantQStrategy.swift:85`, `:127`). RT-5: raw pointers and `AlignedBuffer`. RT-6: the C decimator sets a sample below 10⁻²⁰ to zero as it enters the history (`ztd_rate.c:37` → `ztd_history.c:40-45`, `ztd_flush_state`); an FIR feeds nothing back, so no sub-floor value is held (note `constant-q-transform.md`, hazard 6). RT-4, RT-8, RT-9: not applicable.
  - Allocation audit: `ConstantQTests/theNodeRunsWithoutAllocating()` runs the decimators of a 7-octave and a 6-octave transform for 1 000 blocks of 512 after 8 warm-up blocks: passes in Debug (runs 1, 2) and Release (run 4); with an allocation added in `HalfBandDecimator.process` (`ConstantQNodes.swift:143`, injection 12) it fails — both pipelines of more than one octave report the allocation: `ConstantQTests.swift:383` and the chroma audit, which runs `ChromaCQT`, `ChromaTests.swift:301`; the one-octave pipeline of `:388`, which has no decimator, does not (`mut-12.log`).
  - No hidden copies: the decimator holds a C struct and two pointer cells; `process` takes and gives raw pointers; nothing is captured by a closure (the function pointers are `@convention(c)`).
  - Complexity (§7.2 rows 9 and 13): one 53-tap half-band FIR per octave boundary (CR-022 item 5), run at the input rate of each octave, so the work of all decimators together is below twice that of the first; the output does not depend on how the input is cut (`aDecimatorDoesNotDependOnHowItsInputIsCut…`).
  - Setups at compile time, SEL-3: the filter, the kernel and every buffer are made with the node; the hot path holds no size test. Benchmark: not asked by this row.
  - Currency (ALG): `Docs/Numerics/half-band-decimator.md` opens with the currency check of 2026-11-15 and points to `currency-2026-11.md` row 13 ("Current", one more alternative to register: CR-020 item 3). The record is consistent; no network was available to check the literature itself.
  - Traps (§9.3): B-5.
