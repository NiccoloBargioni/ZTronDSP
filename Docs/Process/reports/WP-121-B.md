## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **A — API, architecture, platform.**
  - Names and shapes (§8.7 ANI-2, ANI-4; the row): an ordered vector of `Double` (`values`, `count`, `isEmpty`), `+`, `−`, `scaled(by:)`, `magnitudeSquared`, `appending(_:)`, `zero`, and `isReplacement` for the difference of two topologies. Frequencies as log₂ and gains in decibels are the gathering stages' business (WP-305), stated in the type's description. The public API equals the committed digest.
  - Immutability, no configuration object: an immutable `struct` (`Hashable`, `Sendable`); the replacement initialiser is `package`, so a client cannot make one by hand.
  - Concurrency, DEP, platform: Swift 6 language mode; no imports; nothing above iOS 15; no UIKit, Combine or `AnyView`. The UI target's SwiftUI side has its own `VectorArithmetic` type (`ZTronDSPUI/Hosting/AnimatableParameters.swift`), so this core type rests on nothing gated (§9.5).
  - VIS-2: the type's description states the arithmetic of unequal lengths and the threading ("an immutable value, usable on any thread"); 6 of its 12 public declarations have no comment of their own (B-28).
