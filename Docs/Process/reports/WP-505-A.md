## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **N — numerical correctness.**

- Definition: §6.4.1, overlap-add with window-sum-of-squares normalisation (`OverlapAdder`, `Sources/ZTronDSPCore/Toolkit/OverlapAdder.swift:49–61`). Each finished sample is divided by the sum of the squared windows that reached it.
- Edge cases:
  - a frame of 48;
  - hop equal to the frame (rectangular);
  - a window that is not constant overlap-add (case e): it is still exact, because the division is by the actual window sum;
  - an impulse (latency);
  - silence, through the stability tests.
- Where the window sum is below the floor (10⁻¹⁰), the output is 0 (NUM-1). That happens only where one window edge alone covers a sample, at the start of a causal stream.
- Precision: single precision throughout, with the floor in single precision.
- STG-1:
  - fluent modifier: `.inverseSTFT(window:normalization:alignment:)` equals the builder (`InverseSTFTTests.swift:128–129`);
  - trailing `isActive:`: `switchedOffItSuspendsWhatFollows`, and `api-digest.sh` counts it;
  - fixtures for O-6: five must-not-compile fixtures `O-06-InverseSTFTAfter…`, and the must-compile `valid/OrderRulesOfTheSpectralFrontEnd.swift` (`:69`, `:101–102`), through `compile-fixtures.sh`;
  - the order matrix is current;
  - latency and settling time: `ColdStartTests.everyNodeStatesItsLatencyAndItsSettlingTime`, `synthesisBeginsWithItsDelayAgain`;

## Findings (VER-4)

| # | Checklist item | Location | Evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-13 | VIS-2 and the documentation of a result: what the reconstruction claim covers | `Sources/ZTronDSPAnalysis/Steps/InverseSTFT.swift:29–30` ("the reconstruction is exact to the round-off of the transform (SNR ≥ 100 dB)"); `Sources/ZTronDSPCore/Toolkit/OverlapAdder.swift:9–10` ("exact wherever the sum is not zero, at the start of the stream as in its steady state") | See the list after this table | non-blocking |

What the evidence for A-13 shows, for case b (causal Hann 4 096/1 024):

- Over the whole output after the delay, the SNR is 46.2 dB.
- Samples 0 to 4 come out as 0: Hann² there is below the floor (8.9·10⁻¹¹ at sample 4).
- Samples 5 to 63 are off by up to 1.8·10⁻³, because the division by a small window sum amplifies the transform's round-off. Samples 64 to 1 023 are off by up to 2.7·10⁻⁵.
- From sample 1 024 on, the error is at most 3·10⁻⁷.

This start is inherent to causal framing, and the acceptance is met where it applies. But neither comment says that the claim excludes the first frame of a causal stream. The overlap-adder's comment says the opposite.

Suggested resolution: say in both comments that, for causal framing, the samples of the first frame are recovered only as well as the first window weighs them.
