## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - `process`, `command` and `reset` are `ZTD_NONBLOCKING`, defined in a target built with function-effect errors (the check bites: `malloc(16)` written into `ztd_gain_process` stops the package build: "function with 'nonblocking' attribute must not call non-'nonblocking' function 'malloc' [-Werror,-Wfunction-effects]"). Read against RT-2: no allocation, no lock, no system call, no logging; every loop is bounded by the frames, the channels or a size fixed at creation.
  - The audit bites: with an allocation the compiler cannot see (a call, from the hot function, to a helper in another file that does `malloc` and `free` and is declared `nonblocking`, as an allow-list entry is; tried in Debug and in Release, with the same counts) in `ztd_alpha_beta_process` it reports 2 000 allocations (two kernels, 1 000 blocks each).
  - Two doubles of state per channel, allocated in `ztd_alpha_beta_create`; no setup; no copy. The choice of output (position or velocity) is tested once per block and channel, outside the sample loop.
  - Cost O(frames): the recurrence in double precision. Benchmark recorded: `kernel.alpha-beta.512` 3032 ns (about 6 ns per sample).
