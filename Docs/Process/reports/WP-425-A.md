## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **N — numerical correctness.** On well-conditioned fits the taps equal `firls` to rounding. That includes length 1, weighted stop bands, and all four responses. The condition test and the fall-back behave as the note says; both named mutations make the named tests fail. On the ill-conditioned fixture the taps are not determined to 1e-9. The normal matrix has s₃/s₀ = 2.3e-12 and s₅₀/s₀ = 2.1e-19, and Cholesky fails (info 5). `dgelsd` applied to SciPy's own matrix gives taps 6.5e-8 (of the largest tap) away from SciPy's `gelsy`. A relative perturbation of the matrix of 1e-16 moves `dgelsd`'s taps by 6.3e-6. So no independent implementation meets 1e-9 there. **A tolerance was nevertheless loosened in the test without a change request** (STG-2). Observed and not a finding: on a 31-tap fit with a gap (0.1, 0.3 and 0.5 of Nyquist), two backward-stable solutions of the same rank-deficient system differ (the taps' norm is 1 055 here and 980 in SciPy). Both are valid least-squares fits.
