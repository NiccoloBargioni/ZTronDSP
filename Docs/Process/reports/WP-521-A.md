## Change requests filed (FIX-4)

**CR-A1 (proposed): VIS-2 and the witnesses of a documented requirement.**

- *What.* VIS-2 reads "Every public symbol is documented with its threading contract: which context may call it, and whether it may allocate." It does not say whether a public witness of a protocol requirement (`typealias Input`/`Output`, `activation`, `lower(_:)` of a `PipelineStage`; `latency(of:…)` and `makeNode(…)` of a `ConstantQStrategy`; `init()` and `norm(of:)` of a `ChromaNormalization`) needs a comment of its own when the requirement is documented, nor whether a sentence on the conforming type covers it.
- *Why.* Earlier verifications read it differently: WP-316-A graded undocumented witnesses as non-blocking; the audit of the first wave (brief, lesson 1) grades any public symbol without a comment as blocking. Every step of M5c (and `STFT`, `MelScale` before them) leaves these witnesses bare, and this verification grades them blocking by lesson 1 (WP-521, 522a, 522b, 523, 524). The rule should say which reading holds, so that the fix is the same everywhere and the next waves do not differ again.
- *Evidence.* `VerifierA/out/doc-scan.txt`: 39 public declarations of this batch without a comment (8 in the files of WP-521/522a/522b, 5 of WP-523, 19 of WP-524, 7 `buildPartialBlock` overloads in `OrderDiagnostics+ConstantQ.swift`); apart from `ConstantQ.channels`, `ChromaCQT.expansion` and the recipes' and steps' initialisers, every one is a witness of `PipelineStage`, `ConstantQStrategy` or `ChromaNormalization`, or an overload that exists for a diagnostic.
- *Proposed wording* (VIS-2, appended): "A witness of a protocol requirement whose documentation states the context and the allocation is covered by it, provided the conforming type's comment says that its witnesses follow the requirements; every other public symbol has a comment of its own. An `@available(*, unavailable)` declaration that exists only to produce a diagnostic carries the diagnostic as its comment."
- *Sections affected.* §5.11 (VIS-2); §11.6 grading of VIS-2 findings; the steps of §6 that conform to `PipelineStage`.
