It would fail: a `malloc`/`free` planted in `ztd_fir_process` (after `ztd_fir.c:32`, mutant 5161) and in `ztd_partitioned_process` (after `ztd_partitioned.c:112`, mutant 5162) each make it fail; restored, `diff -r` clean.

  - SEL-3: the strategy is bound when the stage is lowered: `.automatic` follows the plan's FIR binding for the tap count (`:99-109`), a forced choice is a kernel of its own (`theStrategyNamesTheKernel`). Complexity as §6.7 and the strategy table say (direct O(K) per sample; partitioned, the partition the largest power of two a block holds, `:125-128`).
