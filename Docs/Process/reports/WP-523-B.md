## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - What runs on the analysis thread: `BandedConstantQNode.process` (`Nodes/ConstantQScaleNodes.swift:83-105`) and `DenseConstantQNode.process` (`:126-133`); `reset` does nothing.
  - RT-2: no allocation, lock, log or `await` (read, and the injections above). The banded node runs three loops over bands sorted, when the node is made, by the implementation their length is bound to (`:59-80`): `Vector.Native.dot`, `Vector.Native.dotSIMD`, `Vector.Accelerated.dot`, all static, so there is no dynamic dispatch; the dense node makes one `cblas_sgemv` per frame. RT-3: the weights, offsets, first bins, counts and order (banded) and the matrix (dense) are made in the node's initialiser, in the factory closure that runs when the plan is compiled (`Steps/ConstantQScale.swift:138-142`). RT-5: `AlignedBuffer`s. RT-6: no state. RT-7 layer 2: the leaf loops are the annotated dot products of `ZTronDSPCore` (`Vector.swift:187-191`, `:273-277`); layer 4 clean. RT-1, RT-4, RT-8, RT-9: not applicable.
  - SEL-1 and SEL-3: two implementations of the projection, banded and dense, and three inside the banded one; the dense or banded choice is made in `lower` from the crossover of a band projection (`Steps/ConstantQScale.swift:132`) and the per-band choice in the banded initialiser, so the hot path holds no size test and no branch on the choice. The step reads the crossover of the mel projection: CR-022 item 10 says so (wanted: a primitive of its own); the code does what the change request says. SEL-6: measured above.

  - Setups when the plan is compiled only: yes. Benchmark: none is asked by this row; the crossover is that of the mel projection (CR-022 item 10).
