## Lens N

- **Definition.** The product of a complex CSR row with a split-complex spectrum, Σ (a + jb)(x + jy): recomputed in double from the kernel's own entries (so the comparison isolates the product's rounding from the kernel's thinning, which is WP-518's).
- **Precision.** `Float` products and sums, a dozen terms a row (7 … 14 at the defaults). Right for the package: the error is bounded relative to the terms, nothing is fed back, and 3.6 ε·T at worst is far below the frame-relative tolerances of §6.1 (8·10⁻⁸ of the frame maximum as the transform's floor). Double would cost twice the bandwidth for nothing a caller can see.
- **Edge cases** (`VA_T05`, `VA_Edges`, and the stress test):
  - *empty / length 1*: `applied` to an empty spectrum and to one bin gives 36 zeros; the node's product is only called on spectra of `columnCount` bins (the node refuses a kernel of another length: `ConstantQNode`, hazard 2 of the note).
  - *DC, Nyquist, silence*: the kernel reads bins 51 … 115 at the defaults; spectra with nothing there (silence, DC, Nyquist, denormal) give exact zeros (stress test, run).
  - *full scale*: three scales 2⁸⁰ apart in the stress test; `applied` to ±greatestFiniteMagnitude everywhere gives finite coefficients.
  - *non-finite input*: a NaN or ±∞ bin that no row reads gives finite coefficients; one a row reads propagates (non-finite in, non-finite out; samples are cleaned at the source adapter, NUM-1).
  - *Int and Double arguments*: the product takes none from a client (`SparseKernelProduct` is internal); `applied` takes arrays only.
- **ALG — currency.** `Docs/Numerics/sparse-kernel-product.md` lines 1-3: the check of 2026-11-15 against the register (§7.2 row 13; no alternative registered for the product; Accelerate's complex sparse routines are iOS 18.5 and later, E-11) and the network search in `currency-2026-11.md` row 13: "Current". Checked as a record.
- **ALG — stability note, mutated** (`VerifierA/scripts/mutate.sh`, restored and compared byte for byte):
  - M5 — `Nodes/ConstantQNodes.swift`, the loop of `SparseKernelProduct`: `sumImaginary += a * y + b * x` → `sumImaginary += a * y - b * x`. `theProductOfEveryStressSpectrumIsWithinTheBoundOfItsTerms()` fails with 541 issues ("error <= bound", "worstShare > 0 && worstShare <= 2") and `theSparseProductIsTheDenseProduct(sparsity:)` in all 3 cases (54 issues), as the note says. Restored, `cmp` identical. Log: `.build/logs/mutation-M5.txt`.
- **Tolerances.** None loosened: (2n + 1)·ε·T and two ε·T a row in the stress test, 10⁻⁵ in the parity test, as the note states.
