## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - RT-2, by reading everything that runs on the analysis thread: `STFTNode.process` (`Nodes/STFTNode.swift:75-97`) walks the block in chunks of at most the largest block (`while done < total`), hands each chunk to the framer (WP-503) and, per frame, multiplies it by the taper (`vDSP_vmul`, `:87`), transforms it through the plan (`:88`, WP-502) and emits it (`:90`); `ChannelMixer.sequence` (`:28-42`) returns a pointer into the block, or the mean of the channels in its own scratch (`vDSP_vadd`, `vDSP_vsmul`). Every loop is bounded by the block size and the hop; no allocation, lock, system call, Objective-C message, logging or `await`. Dynamic dispatch: the plan's witness-table call once per frame, never per sample.
  - The audit and the lines changed: `theNodeRunsWithoutAllocating` passes in Debug and Release. A 64-byte allocation at the top of `STFTNode.process` (after `STFTNode.swift:76`, gate `a504`) makes it fail at `STFTTests.swift:256` (2 000 allocations, one per frame of the two taps) and `:261` (1 000). One in the mixing path of the channel mixer only (after `:31`, gate `a504b`) makes the three-channel audit fail at `:261` (1 000) and the multichannel audits of `Level` and `Waveform` with it (`LevelAndWaveformTests.swift:158`, `:259`, 1 000 each), while the mono audits (`STFTTests.swift:256`, `LevelAndWaveformTests.swift:153`, `:254`) pass: the mixing path is reached by the multichannel audits. Restored (`VerifierB/mutations.py restore`), all pass and the tree equals the pristine copy byte for byte.
  - RT-3: the framer, the taper (copied into an `AlignedBuffer`), the padded frame and the mixer's scratch are made in the initialisers (`:61-73`, `:16-25`), inside the step's factory (`Steps/STFT.swift:115-120`), which the plan compiler runs (core `Engine/ExecutionPlan.swift:458`); the window is sampled and its normalisation computed when the step is lowered (`:103-104`). The log of a logged window (WIN-5) is written by the factory (`:116`), on the compiling thread, never by the node.
  - RT-5: `AlignedBuffer` and raw pointers on the path; the `[Float]` taper of the step's key is copied once. RT-6: the state carried between blocks is the framer's ring of input samples and an integer position; nothing decays toward denormals.
  - No hidden copies: a frame is a view into the ring (§7.2 row 2, no copy per hop; WP-503); the one pass per frame is the windowing into the padded buffer, which the transform needs; the mixer copies only to form the mean of several channels. `any FFTPlan` is a class-bound existential (no box); the factory's closure captures the taper and the backend, but runs when the plan is compiled.
  - Complexity: per frame O(N) for the window and O(N log N) for the transform (§7.2 rows 1 and 2).
  - SEL-3: the plan is bound to the transform length and the mixer to the largest block when the plan is compiled; the hot path holds no size test. The window multiply and the mixer call vDSP at every size (finding 2).
  - A benchmark is recorded: none for the step (finding 1).
