## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **S — system integration.** What the system really grants and reports (iOS 27.0 simulator on the host of §3; a device was not available to this verifier):
  - Grant: the thread leaves its quality-of-service class (kernel tier 0, `qos_class_self` 0) and runs at base priority 97. The header says so (ztd_thread.h:20-21).
  - Refusal on a thread that has its class: class and priority untouched, as the row asks.
  - Refusal while an earlier policy is in force: that policy stays (finding 2).
  - `ztd_thread_clear_time_constraint`: the thread ends at time-share, base priority 31, in no class, whether or not a policy was in force (finding 1), and cannot be put back into a class afterwards (`pthread_set_qos_class_self_np` returns EPERM).
  - Idempotence: clearing twice, and asking again after a refusal, behave as the first time.
  - The helper is not hardware-facing; CAP and MIC rules, session ownership and permissions do not apply to it.
