## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **N — numerical correctness.**
  - Definition (§5.11.4, §6.4.1): the window is the last `count` samples; overlap-add divides by the window sum and writes zero below the floor (NUM-1, `OverlapAdder.swift:60`). Both confirmed above.
  - Precision: `Float` storage and accumulation through vDSP (`vDSP_vma`, `vDSP_vadd`); the error grows with the overlap as the bound allows, 3.46 ε at most.
  - Edge cases: a window of 1, batches of 0 and −1 (no change), silence, DC, Nyquist, full scale, a window with zeros (zero output, no NaN), a negative and a tiny window, a NaN sample; a hop of 0, a hop above the frame, an empty frame and a window of the wrong length are typed errors. A count whose double overflows traps (B-11).
  - Tolerances: the history is compared exactly; the tree's absolute 2·10⁻⁵ for overlap-add is loose against the few ε measured here, but it is not what makes the test pass: the implementation meets the verifier's bound of (2⌈size/hop⌉ + 4) ε in all 224 cases, which include the six shapes of the tree's test.
