## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **A — API, architecture, platform.**
  - `DSPEnvironment.latency` is a derived key: a getter, no setter, not among `SettableKeys` (ENV-3). It is positional: a sink's environment holds the latency up to its `Emit` (ENV-4), and `PipelineSession.environment.latency` is the delay of the whole main line, as CR-015 item 1 says and the property's documentation repeats.
  - A stage reports its latency with no code of its own (EXT-2): through the spec it emits (`.fir`, `.decimator`, `.interpolator`), through `AnalysisStep(latency:)`, `CustomKernel(latencyFrames:)` or `CustomProcessor(latencyFrames:)`; `LoweringContext.emit` adds it at the rate of the stage's input.
  - Frame times are content times (CR-015 item 6): a block after a stage with latency is stamped earlier by that latency; described accurately by the change request.
  - Documentation with threading contract: present on `LoweringContext.emit`, `PipelineSession.environment`, `AnalysisStep.latency`.
