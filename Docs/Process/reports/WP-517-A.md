## Lens N

- **Definition.** Independent: `VerifierA/scripts/ref.py` is written from §6.8 alone (it does not import `Tests/Golden/generators/constant_q.py`); figures above.
- **Precision.** `Double` throughout. Right for the package: a length is a ceiling of `Q·f_s/f`, and with `Float`'s 24 bits a value such as 34 683.0x for C1 at 22.05 kHz would round across the integer; `Double` leaves 10⁻¹⁵, and no length of the 11 geometries is within 10⁻⁹ of a tie.
- **Edge cases** (`VA_Edges`, `VA_T01`–`VA_T03`, each trap probe in a process of its own, see `VerifierA/README.md`):
  - *empty, silence, DC, full scale of a signal*: do not apply, the type takes no samples. DC as a frequency: `minimumFrequency` ±0 is refused; the least subnormal is refused ("would be longer than 10¹² samples").
  - *length 1*: one octave of one bin at 8 kHz from 100 Hz gives [100.0] Hz and [80] samples (Q = 1): right.
  - *Nyquist*: a top bin at 11 024.999 Hz at 22.05 kHz is accepted; one at or above it is refused and the error names how many octaves fit (9 octaves at 22.05 kHz: "8 octaves … fit"; 7 at 100 Hz: "not one octave … fits"): right.
  - *largest admitted values*: 32 octaves from 4·10⁻⁶ Hz at 48 kHz gives N₀ = 617 263 511 634 (below the 10¹² guard); 1 200 bins per octave accepted.
  - *Int arguments* (`octaves`, `binsPerOctave`: Int.max, Int.min, 0, −1): all refused with a reason, no trap.
  - *Double arguments* (`minimumFrequency`, `tuning`, `filterScale`, `gamma`, `sampleRate`: ±greatestFiniteMagnitude, least subnormal, ±0, NaN, ±∞): all refused or accepted with a finite geometry, no trap. `gamma` = greatestFiniteMagnitude and `filterScale` = subnormal give atoms of 1 sample, which is the formula.
  - *the index of `frequency(ofBin:)` and `atomLength(ofBin:)`* (documented: "The formula holds for any index, also outside 0 ..< binCount"; probed with Int.max, Int.min, 0, −1, 252, 36 900, −39 600, −1 800, −1 700 for B = 36, 64, 1): **traps and +∞, finding A-1**.
