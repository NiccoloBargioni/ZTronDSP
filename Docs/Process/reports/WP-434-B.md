| 1 (B-1) | STG-1 ("its latency and settling time"), SWAP-2, ACT-7; a result is wrong | `Sources/CZTronDSP/ztd_alpha_beta.c:107` (`.settling_frames = 0`); the kernel is created by `AlphaBeta.lower` | **What the contract requires.** The kernel contract defines `settling_frames` as "Frames the kernel needs, after a reset, before its output is what it would have been had it always been running: the pre-roll length (SWAP-2, ACT-7)" (`ztd_kernel.h`). **What the tracker needs.** It is a two-pole recurrence: for α = 0.002 and β = 2·10⁻⁶, the roots of z² − (2 − α − β)z + (1 − α) have a radius of 0.998999499. It needs 11 502 frames (240 ms at 48 kHz) to decay by 100 dB, the measure `ztd_biquad_settling` uses, and declares 0. So it is neither pre-rolled when it enters the main line nor when its switch is restored. **Measured by the verifier, on a constant 0.5 at 48 kHz with blocks of 64.** (a) An update from `LowPass(5 kHz)` to `AlphaBeta(alpha: 0.002, beta: 0.000002)` at sample 9 600: the output falls to 0.3468 at sample 9 929, a deviation of 0.1532, and stays more than 10⁻³ away until sample 15 844, 6 244 samples after the change. (b) Restoring a bypassed `AlphaBeta` the same way: it falls to 0.3463, a deviation of 0.1537. **The control.** `LowPass(10 Hz, order 2)`, which declares its settling time, deviates by less than 10⁻⁴ in both cases.
