## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - The delay line and the buffer of the unprocessed block are allocated in `ztd_plan_make_switchable` (control context), only for a kernel with latency. `ztd_delay_feed`, `ztd_delay_run`, `ztd_fade_block` and `ztd_entry_run` are `ZTD_NONBLOCKING`: `memcpy` and one integer remainder per block, no loop that is not bounded by the channels. `malloc(16)` written into `ztd_gain_process` stops the package build: "function with 'nonblocking' attribute must not call non-'nonblocking' function 'malloc' [-Werror,-Wfunction-effects]".
  - Allocation audit: `SwitchTests.aSwitchedPlanIsAllocationFreeInEveryState` uses a stage with a latency of 100 frames and walks it through every state for 1 000 blocks; passes in Debug and Release. It bites: with an allocation the compiler cannot see (a call, from the hot function, to a helper in another file that does `malloc` and `free` and is declared `nonblocking`, as an allow-list entry is; tried in Debug and in Release, with the same counts) in `ztd_delay_feed` (and `ztd_crossfade_mix`) it reports 1 143 allocations.
  - Cost while the stage is active (ACT-8, §5.7 "the above plus one copy of the block"), measured by the verifier's probe on a gain kernel, 512 frames, Release: no switch 33.8 ns; switch, no latency 34.0 ns; switch with a latency of 100 frames 41.6 ns; of 1 000 frames 68.8 ns; a bare copy of 512 frames 27.7 ns. The claim holds: at most one copy of the block.
  - No setup; nothing bound by size.
