## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - RT-2: reading and writing through a block and copying or clearing one allocate nothing and take no lock (the audits above); there is no dynamic dispatch: every accessor is `@inlinable`, and the disassembly shows them inlined.
  - RT-3: an `AudioBuffer` allocates when it is created, with overflow-checked sizes and a typed error for what cannot be had (`AudioBuffer(channelCount: Int.max, capacity: Int.max)` throws `resourceAllocationFailed`, crash probe `audioBufferHuge`), and frees in `deinit`; its documentation puts both on the control context.
  - RT-5: the storage is one `AlignedBuffer` and a raw table of channel pointers; `Array` appears only in `AudioBuffer(channels:)` and `array(ofChannel:)`, both documented as allocating and neither on the hot path.
  - No hidden copies: a block is a view of the buffer's table (`sampleFramesWrapABlock` and `blockLayoutIsPlanar` compare addresses); `copy(from:)` is the one copy and is explicit.
  - RT-7(2): `clear()` and `copy(from:)` loop over channels and call `memset` and `memcpy`, so they are not pure-Swift leaf loops, which are the only loops the attributes can be put on (E-05).
  - Complexity: no row of §7.2 applies; copy and clear are linear in the samples, by `memcpy` and `memset`.
  - Benchmark: the row asks for none. The one figure in the file (about 260 ns per block, `AudioBlock.swift:113-116`) is the page offset added by WP-324, a later package, and is not this row's claim; it was not checked here.
  - Setups, SEL-3: not applicable; the package creates no setup and has one implementation.
