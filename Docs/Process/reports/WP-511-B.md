## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - RT-2, by reading everything that runs on the analysis thread: `LifterNode.process` (`Nodes/CepstrumNodes.swift:186-190`) is one bound multiply by the weights and one emission. `CepstralPitchNode.process` (`:212-241`): the index of the largest value in the range (`vDSP_maxvi`), a parabola through it and its neighbours in double precision, the mean magnitude (`vDSP_meamgv`), the confidence clamped to 0 … 1, one emission; a frame without a positive peak repeats the last value with confidence 0. No loop of their own, no allocation, lock, system call, Objective-C message, logging or `await`.
  - The audit and the lines changed: `theNodesRunWithoutAllocating` passes in Debug and Release. A 64-byte allocation in `CepstralPitchNode.process`, after the guard (before `CepstrumNodes.swift:215`, gate `a511`), makes it fail at `CepstrumTests.swift:327` with 1 000 allocations (the one pitch); one before the lifter's multiply (before `:188`, gate `a511b`) with 3 000 (the three lifters). Restored, it passes and the tree equals the pristine copy byte for byte.
  - RT-3: the lifter's weights are computed when the step is lowered (`Steps/Cepstrum.swift:209-226`), copied once into an `AlignedBuffer` in the node's initialiser (`:181-184`) and the multiply bound by the cepstrum's length (`Steps/Cepstrum.swift:227`); the pitch's range is resolved to quefrencies when the step is lowered (`:265-288`) and the node holds three numbers; both nodes are made in their factories (`:228-229`, `:289-292`).
  - RT-4: the measuring spelling is its `Tap { CepstralPitch(…); Emit(to:) }` expansion: its value reaches the binding through the core's result path. RT-5: `AlignedBuffer` and raw pointers. RT-6: the only state is the pitch's last value, an output.
  - No hidden copies: the lifter writes the product straight into the output frame, the pitch reads the cepstrum in place; `Vector.Bound` is a struct of non-capturing closures.
  - Complexity: O(N) per frame for the lifter and O(range) for the pitch (the family of §7.2 row 19).
  - SEL-3: the lifter's multiply is bound by the length when the plan is compiled; the pitch's `vDSP_maxvi` and `vDSP_meamgv` (an index of the maximum, a mean magnitude) are not primitives of §7.4. The hot paths hold no size test.
  - A benchmark is recorded: none (finding 1).
