## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - RT-2 and RT-5: the pointer form `zeroPad` is documented "Allocation-free; callable on the analysis thread" and is (above); the array form `zeroPadded` allocates and says so ("not for the analysis thread").
  - Setups (E-09): never created by the rule; the tree's test creates setups for admitted lengths only, which is what the rule is for.
  - RT-7(2): no pure-Swift leaf loop runs on the analysis thread here: `zeroPad` calls `memset` and `memcpy`, which the attributes do not accept (E-05), and `isFastLength` has no loop. `nextFastLength` and `previousFastLength` loop, but they are used when a plan is compiled.
  - Complexity: `isFastLength` constant; `nextFastLength` and `previousFastLength` at most four times the number of doublings (about 60).
  - Benchmark: the row asks for none.
  - SEL-3: one implementation.
  - COV-3: `Padding.swift` 100 % (50 of 50 lines) under the tree's 46 suites.
