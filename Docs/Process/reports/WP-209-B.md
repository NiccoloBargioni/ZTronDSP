## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - The update is one call of `vDSP_biquad_SetCoefficientsDouble` on the existing setup (ztd_biquad.c:124-128), or a copy of 5·sections floats in the C loop (:130-135): no allocation, no setup created, the delay values untouched. Both are `ZTD_NONBLOCKING`; the vDSP setter is on the allow-list with its audit named.
  - Allocation audit: `processingAndCoefficientUpdatesAreAllocationFree(bound:)` sends 50 updates among 1 000 blocks; passes in Debug and Release. It bites on the update path itself: with an allocation the compiler cannot see (a call, from the hot function, to a helper in another file that does `malloc` and `free` and is declared `nonblocking`, as an allow-list entry is; tried in Debug and in Release, with the same counts) in the two `command` functions (and in `process`) the count is 1 050, that is 1 000 blocks plus the 50 updates.
  - The update is applied where the plan applies commands, between two blocks (`ztd_plan_command`). The payload stays the control context's (ztd_kernel.h:34-37) and is read, not kept.
  - Cost: O(sections); measured by the verifier's probe at 46 ns for two sections (Release).
