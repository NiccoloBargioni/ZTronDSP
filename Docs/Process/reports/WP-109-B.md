## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **N — numerical correctness.**
  - Definition (§5.2 "Scopes", ENV-1): the rate downstream of a change is `rate·L/M`, Nyquist half the rate; inside a spectral scope the rate is that of the framed signal; a feature sequence restarts at the feature rate. All confirmed above against exact values.
  - Precision: `Double`; `rate · L / M` rounds twice (worst 0.44 ulp seen), so a rate taken down and up by a factor that does not divide it is not always given back (B-22).
  - Edge cases: a frame, a hop or a decimation of 0, and a non-finite rate, give infinities and NaN that are stored in the environment and read by the next stage (B-5). DC, silence and full scale do not apply to this row.
  - No tolerance was loosened: the tests compare with `==`.
