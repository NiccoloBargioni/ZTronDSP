## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - `ztd_crossfade_begin`, `ztd_crossfade_is_done` and `ztd_crossfade_mix` are `ZTD_NONBLOCKING` (ztd_kernels.h:194-201); the state is two integers the caller owns; nothing is allocated, ever. `malloc(16)` written into `ztd_gain_process` stops the package build: "function with 'nonblocking' attribute must not call non-'nonblocking' function 'malloc' [-Werror,-Wfunction-effects]".
  - The audit bites: with an allocation the compiler cannot see (a call, from the hot function, to a helper in another file that does `malloc` and `free` and is declared `nonblocking`, as an allow-list entry is; tried in Debug and in Release, with the same counts) in `ztd_crossfade_mix` it reports 1 000 allocations, and the audits of its two users fail with it (the FIR's tap fade: 1 376; the plan's switch: 1 143).
  - The weight of a frame is computed from its place in the fade, not accumulated: the loop is bounded by the frames of the block, with one copy (`memmove`) of what lies past the end of the fade when `out` is not `to`.
  - Cost O(frames): measured by the verifier's probe at 357 ns for 512 frames (Release), 0.7 ns per frame.
