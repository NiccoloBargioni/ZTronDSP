## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - RT-2, by reading everything that runs on the analysis thread: `BandedMelNode.process` (`Nodes/MelNodes.swift:82-104`) runs three `while` loops over the bands in an order fixed at creation, one dot product per band: the scalar loop, the 8-lane loop (`Vector.Native.dot`, `dotSIMD`, annotated `@_noAllocation @_noLocks` behind `ZTRONDSP_PERF_ANNOTATIONS`) and vDSP (`Vector.Accelerated.dot`), all static calls; together the loops visit each band once. `DenseMelNode.process` (`:124-130`) is one `cblas_sgemv`. One emission each. No allocation, lock, system call, Objective-C message, logging or `await` that the verifier could see; whether `cblas_sgemv` hands work to other threads is not visible to the audit, which counts the calling thread (not verified).
  - The audit and the lines changed: `theNodesRunWithoutAllocating` passes in Debug and Release. A 64-byte allocation in `BandedMelNode.process` (before `MelNodes.swift:86`, gate `a535` of `VerifierB/mutations.py`) makes the banded audit fail at `MelTests.swift:234` with 2 000 allocations (the two banded nodes) and leaves the dense audit passing; one before the `cblas_sgemv` of `DenseMelNode` (before `:128`, gate `a535b`) makes the dense audit fail at `:239` with 2 000 (one per frame at a hop of 256). Each audit reaches its own implementation. Restored, both pass and the tree equals the pristine copy byte for byte.
  - RT-3: the filter bank is built and checked (O-18) when the step is lowered (`Steps/MelScale.swift:69-93`); the banded node packs its weights, first bins, counts and the order of the bands by implementation once, in its initialiser (`MelNodes.swift:27-80`), and the dense node copies the bank's matrix once (`:118-122`); the factory (`Steps/MelScale.swift:105-110`) is run by the plan compiler.
  - RT-5: `AlignedBuffer` throughout; the bank's arrays are read only when the node is made. RT-6: no state.
  - No hidden copies: the spectrum is read in place at each band's first bin; the bank is a value used at creation only; no closure, no existential on the path.
  - Complexity (§7.2 row 18): banded triangles, each bin in at most two bands, so O(bins) per frame; the dense product, O(bands × bins), only above the crossover, as registered.
  - SEL-3: banded or dense is decided when the step is lowered, by bands × bins against the engine's crossover (`Steps/MelScale.swift:103`, `Primitive.melProjection`; shipped `melDense` 2²⁰, core `Toolkit/Implementation.swift:84`), and within the banded node each band's dot product is bound by its run length when the node is made (`MelNodes.swift:58-79`); the loops hold no size test. The crossover is "to be measured" in §7.4, by WP-611c and WP-611d (`AnalysisCalibrationTests` measures `melDense`).
