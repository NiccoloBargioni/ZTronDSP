## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - RT-2, by reading everything that runs on the analysis thread: the complex branch of `CepstrumNode.process` (`Nodes/CepstrumNodes.swift:61-87`): `vvatan2`, a `while` loop that unwraps the phase along the bins (`:68-75`), and one that takes out the linear-phase ramp (`:84-87`), both bounded by the bin count; then the inverse transform and the trailer of two values (`:102-105`). `InverseCepstrumNode.process` (`:139-168`): to double precision, the double forward transform, a `while` loop that restores the ramp (`:153-156`), `vvexp`, `vvsincos`, `vDSP_vsmulD`, two `vDSP_vmulD`, back to single precision, two stores, one emission. No allocation, lock, system call, Objective-C message, logging or `await`.
  - The audit and the line changed: `theNodesRunWithoutAllocating` passes in Debug and Release. A 64-byte allocation in `InverseCepstrumNode.process`, before its first conversion (before `CepstrumNodes.swift:145`, gate `a510` of `VerifierB/mutations.py`), makes it fail at `CepstrumTests.swift:327` with 1 000 allocations, one per frame of the one inverse; the complex branch of `CepstrumNode` is under gate `a509` (WP-509-B, 3 000). Restored, it passes and the tree equals the pristine copy byte for byte.
  - RT-3: the double transform and six double buffers of the inverse are made in its initialiser (`:127-137`), inside the step's factory (`Steps/Cepstrum.swift:162-166`); the layout of a complex frame (M + 2 values, CR-019 item 6) is checked when the step is lowered (`:153-161`).
  - RT-5: `AlignedBuffer` throughout. RT-6: neither node keeps state between frames.
  - No hidden copies: the conversions are the precision of §6.1; the phase is unwrapped in place; no closure, no copy-on-write.
  - Complexity (§7.2 row 19): per frame one transform, O(N log N), and O(N) for the logarithm, the phase and the exponential, as registered.
  - SEL-3: double precision, for which §7.4 registers no second implementation; the transform is bound to its length when the plan is compiled.
  - RT-7, layer 2: the three loops above are pure Swift but sit inside a `process` that calls C, which `@_noAllocation` rejects (E-05); they are covered by the run-time audit only (finding 2).
  - A benchmark is recorded: none (finding 1).
