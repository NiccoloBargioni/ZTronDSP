## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **N — numerical correctness.**

- Definitions: §6.13, the STFT row. The conditions are:
  - non-zero overlap-add, Σ w²(n − kH) > 0, necessary;
  - constant overlap-add, for a flat response, not necessary.
- The error when it fails is amplified by B/A. B/A is the frame-bound ratio of `OverlapAdd.frameBounds`, the same figure `Window.analysis` reports.
- Edge cases:
  - hop equal to the frame, with a window that is zero at its ends (fails) and one that is not (rectangular, holds);
  - a window that is zero over part of every hop;
  - hops that leave B/A at 2·10⁵ (the prediction then says nothing: 1);
  - centred framing;
  - a frame or hop of 0 is a typed error, not a report (`aReportCanBeCancelledAndRefusesNonsense`).
- The round trip leaves out the first frame of a causal analysis and the end that no frame covers, as its comment says (`StageInversions.swift:46–48`). This is the reading of WP-505's acceptance.
- Precision: the frame bounds in double precision, from the window sampled in double precision. The measured error is limited to about 140 dB by single precision (`inversion-analysis.md`, hazard 2).
- CR-019 item 11 records that the measured error grows as √(B/A), while the bound keeps B/A. In the table every measured error lies well below its bound: by a factor of 67 for a tight frame and 1.8·10⁴ for Kaiser(14).
- No tolerance was loosened.
