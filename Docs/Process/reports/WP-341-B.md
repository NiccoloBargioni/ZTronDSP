## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - RT-2 / RT-4: a snapshot never blocks the writer: it copies the ring and then checks the writer's two counters; columns begun meanwhile are dropped from the front, a clear makes it start over (at most 16 times). The writer's path is untouched by a snapshot in progress (the writer of the verifier's stress needed 10 ms for 300 000 columns in Release beside 100 000 snapshots).
  - It allocates and copies, by design: two arrays of the size of the history (512 × 512 values: 51 µs, Release, indicative). BG-2: an immutable `Sendable` value comes back.
  - Complexity: linear in the history. The arrays are zero-filled before they are overwritten (one extra pass).
  - `FeedSnapshot.image` (BG-1) belongs to the Metal target and is not part of this package.
  - A benchmark is recorded: none.
