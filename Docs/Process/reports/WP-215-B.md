## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - `process`, `command` and `reset` are `ZTD_NONBLOCKING`, defined in a target built with function-effect errors (the check bites: `malloc(16)` written into `ztd_gain_process` stops the package build: "function with 'nonblocking' attribute must not call non-'nonblocking' function 'malloc' [-Werror,-Wfunction-effects]"). Read against RT-2: no allocation, no lock, no system call, no logging; every loop is bounded by the frames, the channels or a size fixed at creation.
  - The audit bites: with an allocation the compiler cannot see (a call, from the hot function, to a helper in another file that does `malloc` and `free` and is declared `nonblocking`, as an allow-list entry is; tried in Debug and in Release, with the same counts) in `ztd_partitioned_process` it reports 1 000 allocations.
  - No setup and no buffer is created while processing; `vDSP_DFT_Execute` and `vDSP_conv` are on the allow-list.
  - Cost for blocks that bring whole partitions: two transforms and K / B spectral products per partition, the registered O(log B + K/B) per sample. `kernel.partitioned.2001-taps.partition-512.512` 3126 ns against `kernel.fir.2001-taps.512` 33477 ns for the direct kernel: about ten times faster. Benchmarks recorded.
  - Copies: the input partition into the window, the window split into even and odd samples for `vDSP_DFT_zrop`, the window moved up by one partition: inherent in the overlap-save form.
