## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - RT-2, by reading everything that runs on the analysis thread: `InverseSTFTNode.process` (`Nodes/InverseSTFTNode.swift:52-86`), per frame: the plan's inverse (`:56`), the normalisation's scale when it is not 1 (`vDSP_vsmul`, `:59`), the overlap-adder's `add` (core `Toolkit/OverlapAdder.swift:49-72`: `vDSP_vma`, `vDSP_vadd`, a `while` loop over one hop, two `memmove`, two `memset`), one `memcpy` of the finished hop into the output block (`:84`) and its emission. After a reset the first frame emits the delay as zeros, hop by hop (`:74-83`): at most ⌈delay / hop⌉ ≤ ⌈frame / hop⌉ passes, once per cold start. No allocation, lock, system call, Objective-C message, logging or `await`; the plan's witness-table call once per frame.
  - The audit and the line changed: `theNodeRunsWithoutAllocating` passes in Debug and Release. A 64-byte allocation before the inverse transform (before `InverseSTFTNode.swift:56`, gate `a505` of `VerifierB/mutations.py`, run by `VerifierB/run-gates.sh a505`) makes it fail at `InverseSTFTTests.swift:160`: 3 000 allocations, one per frame of the two round trips (2 000 + 1 000). Restored, it passes and the tree equals the pristine copy byte for byte.
  - RT-3: the overlap-adder (its window, the squared window, the sums and the window sums, `OverlapAdder.swift:31-45`), the time-domain frame and the finished hop are made in the initialiser (`:31-50`), inside the step's factory (`Steps/InverseSTFT.swift:119-122`), which the plan compiler runs; the window is sampled and the overlap-add condition evaluated when the step is lowered (`:103-111`); the plan as in WP-502.
  - RT-5: `AlignedBuffer` throughout. RT-6: the state carried between frames is the sums of the frames that still overlap (at most ⌈frame / hop⌉ of them, no recursion), the drop count and a flag: nothing decays toward denormals.
  - No hidden copies: one copy of a hop per frame from the adder's output into the block (it may start inside the hop, by the samples a centred analysis reflected); no closure on the path, no copy-on-write; `any FFTPlan` is class-bound.
  - Complexity: per frame O(N log N) for the transform and O(frame) for the overlap-add (§7.2 row 1).
  - SEL-3: the plan is bound to its length, the delay and the drop count are fixed when the plan is compiled; no size test on the hot path. The normalisation's scale calls vDSP at every frame size (finding 2).
  - A benchmark is recorded: none (finding 1).
