## Lens checklist (§11.5)

- **R — real-time safety and performance.**
  - RT-2, by reading everything that runs on the analysis thread: `LevelNode.process` (`Nodes/LevelAndWaveformNodes.swift:30-60`) walks the block in chunks through the channel mixer and the framer (WP-503, as `STFT` does) and reduces each window to one value: the bound mean square (`:45`) or `vDSP_maxmgv` (`:48`), then `DSP.decibels(fromAmplitude:)` or `(fromPower:)` (core `Units/Amplification.swift:96-106`: `abs`, `log10`, `max`) and one emission. Loops bounded by the block size and the hop; no allocation, lock, system call, Objective-C message, logging or `await`; the bound reduction is one call through a stored non-capturing closure per window.

  - RT-3: the framer and the mixer's scratch are made in the initialisers (`:20-28`; `Nodes/STFTNode.swift:16-25`) inside the step's factory (`Steps/Level.swift:61-66`); the mean square is bound by the window length when the step is lowered (`Vector.Bound`, `:59`); windows are limited to 2²⁸ samples (`:52`).
